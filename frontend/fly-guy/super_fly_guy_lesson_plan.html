Overview

故事概览

Story Summary

One day, Fly Guy goes to school with Buzz. He learns about reading, phonics, and art. When lunchtime comes, Fly Guy loves the lunchroom — he loves the dirty dishes, the smelly mop, and the garbage cans. He meets the lunch lady, whose name is Roz. "This fly is smart!" she says. "He knows my name!" Roz feeds Fly Guy chicken bones and fish heads in sour milk, and Fly Guy is very happy.

But Roz's boss is not happy. "The children cannot eat in a room full of flies!" he says. "You are fired!" Roz is sad, and so is everyone else, because Roz is a good cook. The next day, Miss Muzzle becomes the new lunch lady. She makes burnt peas and turnips. No one in school eats lunch — not even Fly Guy, who eats almost anything. Everyone misses Roz, even the boss.

That night, Buzz makes a plan. The next day, Fly Guy boinks Miss Muzzle right on the nose. She grabs her swatter and swings — but she misses. She misses again and again, and she makes a terrible mess. The boss is not happy. "You are fired!" he says. The next day, Roz is back! "You are a super Fly Guy!" she cheers, and she makes a special garbage soup just for him.

📖 文本特征

  • 3 chapters, ~30 pages, easy reader
  • 重复句式：He loved the ___. / ___ was not happy. / "You are fired!"
  • 修辞手法：反复 (repetition)、夸张 (hyperbole)、拟声 (boink)
  • 核心主题词：lunchroom, saving the day, friendship, being a helper

🎯 教学目标

  • Reading: 理解"问题–计划–解决"结构，追踪角色动机
  • Language: 掌握核心词汇、过去时态、because 原因表达、was/were 情感描述
  • Speaking: 能复述故事、对比两位 lunch lady
  • Writing: 完成 opinion 写作：Was Miss Muzzle a good cook?
Language Target Framework

LT 框架总览 — Super Fly Guy

以下 8 个模块构成本书的完整语言目标体系，两个课时分工推进。

LT01

Vocabulary 词汇

12 个核心词，含 IPA、词性、搭配、例句、词族。

  • lunchroom, lunch lady, mop, garbage
  • smelly, smart, sour, burnt
  • cook, fired, swatter, mess, plan
LT02

Phonics / Decoding 语音拼读

  • sm- blend: smart, smelly
  • gr- blend: grabbed, garbage
  • sh digraph: fish, she, dishes, wash
  • -oo vowels: school, room, good, cook
  • -ed past suffix: loved, learned, missed
LT03

Pronunciation 发音

  • 音节切分：lunch-room (2), gar-bage (2), smel-ly (2), swat-ter (2)
  • 重音模式：LUNCH-room, GAR-bage, SWAT-ter, MISS-tress
  • 姓名的重音：Roz, Miss MUZ-zle
  • 连读：fed_the fly, grabbed_her swatter
LT04

Sentence Patterns 句型

  • [Subject] loved the [noun].
  • [Name] was the [job].
  • [Subject] made [food].
  • ___ was / were not happy.
  • "You are fired!"
LT05

Grammar 语法功能

  • Describe with be: was / were + 情感形容词
  • Reason: because — "because Roz was a good cook."
  • Negation: cannot / cannot + verb
  • Simple past (故事叙述): went, met, said, made, swung
  • Possessives: my, her, his
LT06

Morphology 词形变化

  • -ed 规则过去式: loved, learned, asked, missed
  • 不规则过去式: went, met, was, said, made, fed, swung
  • -y 形容词后缀: smell→smelly, dirt→dirty, mess→messy
  • 复合词: lunchroom, lunch lady, garbage soup
  • 不规则复数: dish→dishes, box→boxes
LT07

Cohesion / Discourse 语篇衔接

  • because (原因): "sad because Roz was a good cook"
  • and: 并列连接
  • then / the next day: 时间推进
  • 代词指代: he / she / her / they
  • 关系代词 who: "Fly Guy, who ate almost anything."
  • even: "not even Fly Guy" / "Even the boss missed Roz."
LT08

Functional Communication 语用功能

  • Describe: What does Fly Guy love in the lunchroom?
  • Retell: What happened first / next / at the end?
  • Compare: Roz vs. Miss Muzzle — who is a better cook?
  • Express opinion: Was Miss Muzzle a good cook?
  • Predict: What will Fly Guy do next?

Chapter 1 精读 (4 min)

页面原文教学重点
p.1–3 "One day Fly Guy went to school with Buzz. Fly Guy learned about reading and phonics. He learned about art." 引入简单过去时 as 叙述时态；讨论 Fly Guy 在学校学了什么

Chapter 2 精读 (3 min)

页面原文教学重点
p.12–14 "Roz's boss was not happy. 'The children cannot eat in a room full of flies!' he said. 'You are fired!'" 理解 cannot / fired；讨论老板为什么生气
p.15–16 "Roz was sad. Fly Guy was sad. Buzz and the children were sad because Roz was a good cook." 情感形容词 + because 原因表达
p.17–19 "The next day, Roz was gone. Miss Muzzle was the new lunch lady. She made burnt peas and turnips." 理解 gone / burnt / turnips；对比新旧 lunch lady
p.20–21 "No one in school ate lunch—not even Fly Guy, who ate almost anything. Everyone missed Roz. Even the boss missed Roz." 学习 even / who 从句；体会夸张手法

Chapter 3 精读 (3 min)

页面原文教学重点
p.24–25 "Miss Muzzle looked up. Fly Guy boinked her on the nose. Miss Muzzle cried, 'No flies in my lunchroom!'" 拟声词 boink；命令式 "No flies in my lunchroom!"
p.26–29 "She grabbed her swatter and swung. She missed. She missed again. She missed again." 反复 She missed again；理解 swatter / swung
p.30 "The boss was not happy. 'The children cannot eat in this mess,' he said. 'You are fired!'" 前后呼应 "You are fired!"；理解 mess

⏱ 33–43 min · LT02 Phonics + LT03 Pronunciation
语音拼读与发音训练

Phonics Focus (5 min)

从本书中提取高频语音模式，引导学生发现规律：

辅音连缀 Consonant Blends

sm- smart, smelly gr- grabbed, garbage

sh 字母组合

sh /ʃ/ — fish, she, dishes, wash

这个音出现在很多与"脏碗盘"相关的词里，正好符合本书的午餐室主题。

-oo 两个读音

oo /uː/ — school, room, lunchroom oo /ʊ/ — good, cook

同样的拼写有两个读音："Roz is a good cook" 用短音 /ʊ/；"school" 和 "lunchroom" 用长音 /uː/。听音辨词！

-ed 过去式后缀

-ed /d/ — loved, learned, cried -ed /t/ — missed

-ed 有三种读音。本书故事用过去时叙述，正好练习听辨 /d/, /t/。

⏱ 5–18 min · LT05 Grammar
语法功能深度分析

① was / were + 情感形容词 — 描述感受 (4 min)

原文句功能结构
"Fly Guy was happy."描述情绪（单数）主语(单数) + was + 形容词
"Roz was sad. Fly Guy was sad."描述情绪（单数）主语(单数) + was + 形容词
"Buzz and the children were sad."描述情绪（复数）主语(复数) + were + 形容词
"The boss was not happy."否定情绪主语 + was not + 形容词

练习：用 was / were 描述故事角色。 "Roz was ___. The children were ___."

② because — 说明原因 (4 min)

[结果], because [原因].
"Buzz and the children were sad because Roz was a good cook."

because 表示原因，用法：先结果，后原因。这是本书推动情节的关键连接词。

练习："Why was everyone sad?" → "Everyone was sad because ___."

③ cannot — 否定能力/允许 (3 min)

原文句功能结构
"The children cannot eat in a room full of flies!"不允许（否定）主语 + cannot + 动词原形
"The children cannot eat in this mess!"不允许（否定，呼应用例）主语 + cannot + 动词原形

cannot（正式）常缩略为 can't。对比第一本 Hi! Fly Guy 中的 can / can't 能力表达，这里更强调"规定/允许"。注意文中用了正式写法 cannot。

④ 所有格代词 my / her / his (3 min)

原文句物主说明
"He knows my name!"my说话者 Roz 的（第一人称）
"Her name was Roz."her指 Roz 的（女性第三人称）
"She grabbed her swatter."her指 Miss Muzzle 的
"His name..."his男性第三人称（复习/拓展）
⚠️ 重点提示 本书有大量不规则过去式（went, met, was, said, made, fed, swung）。这些词不能靠规则推导，需要单独记忆。建议用配对练习：给出 base form，学生快速说出 past tense。

⏱ 26–40 min · LT08 Functional Communication
口语输出与写作任务

Activity 1: Retell the Story (5 min)

学生两人一组，用以下框架复述故事：

Story Retelling Frame:

First, Fly Guy went to school with Buzz and learned about reading and phonics.
Next, Fly Guy loved the lunchroom and met the lunch lady, Roz.
Then, Roz got fired because there were flies in the lunchroom.
After that, Miss Muzzle made burnt peas and turnips, and everyone missed Roz.
In the end, Fly Guy boinked Miss Muzzle on the nose, she made a mess, and Roz came back. Fly Guy was a SUPER hero!

Activity 2: Express Your Opinion (5 min)

回到 Big Question，进行 mini-debate：

Discussion Prompts:

  • "Was Miss Muzzle a good cook? Why or why not?"
  • "Was it fair to fire Roz? Why do you think so?"
  • "Was Fly Guy a hero? What did he do to help?"

Language scaffold:

"I think Miss Muzzle was a ___ because ___."
"I agree / disagree because ___."
"In my opinion, ___."

Activity 3: Writing — Opinion Piece (4 min 指导 + 课后完成)

Writing Prompt:

Should Fly Guy be allowed to stay in the lunchroom? Write your opinion. Give at least two reasons.

Writing Frame (scaffold):

I think Fly Guy should be allowed in the lunchroom because ___.
First, ___.
Also, ___.
In conclusion, ___.
