Overview

故事概览

Story Summary

Buzz has a pet fly named Fly Guy who can say the boy's name — "BUZZ!" One day Dad asks, "Who wants to go for a ride?" Buzz and Fly Guy climb in the car with the windows rolled down. Wind blows in, picks Fly Guy up, and carries him right out the window!

Buzz chases his little friend across every kind of transport: "Follow that truck!" Fly Guy tumbles into a truck driver's mouth, gets spit into a passing motorboat, jumps overboard onto a circus train, surprises a sleepy elephant, is blown onto an airplane, and just as the pilot turns on the wipers — a rocket roars up from the ground! "Follow that rocket!" But Fly Guy is safe: the rocket didn't take him to space. Dad says, "Let's ride home." And Fly Guy says — "MORE RIDEZZZ!"

📖 文本特征

  • 3 chapters, structure by one chasing scene after another
  • 重复句式：Follow that ___! (truck / boat / train / airplane / rocket)
  • 关键修辞手法：反复 refrain、悬念 (will he survive?)、幽默结尾
  • 核心主题词：transport, chase, going on a ride, prepositions of movement

🎯 教学目标

  • Reading: 按事件顺序理解追逐情节，提取每站的交通工具
  • Language: 掌握祈使句 Follow that ___!、交通工具词、方向介词 (out of / into / onto / overboard)、过去时
  • Speaking: 能下达指令、按顺序复述追逐过程
  • Writing: 完成一段自创追逐小故事 (chase story)
Language Target Framework

LT 框架总览 — Ride, Fly Guy, Ride!

以下 8 个模块构成本书的完整语言目标体系，两个课时分工推进。

LT01

Vocabulary 词汇

12 个核心词，含 IPA、词性、搭配、例句、词族。

  • ride, follow, truck, boat, train
  • airplane, rocket, window, overboard
  • space, wind, survive
LT02

Phonics / Decoding 语音拼读

  • -air: airplane, air
  • -ow /oʊ/: window, blow, follow
  • -oa /oʊ/: boat, road
  • cr-: cried  bl-: blew, blown  sp-: spit, space
  • r-controlled ar: car, start
LT03

Pronunciation 发音

  • 音节切分：follow (2), window (2), airplane (2), rocket (2)
  • 重音模式：FOLLOW, WINdow, AIRplane, ROCKet, o-ver-BOARD? (overboard 首重 OV-er-board)
  • 祈使句句重音："FOLLOW that TRUCK!"
  • -ing 连音：pass-ing, go-ing, rid-ing
LT04

Sentence Patterns 句型

  • Follow that [vehicle]! (祈使句)
  • [Wind] carried [obj] out of / into / onto ___.
  • [S] jumped overboard / blew him onto ___.
  • Will [s.o.] + verb? — "Will Fly Guy survive?"
  • 重复 chaining: truck → boat → train → airplane → rocket
LT05

Grammar 语法功能

  • Imperatives: Follow that ___! / Buckle up! / Let's ride home.
  • Simple past: blew, carried, cried, tumbled, jumped, surprised, roared, landed
  • Prepositions of movement: out of, into, onto, overboard, up
  • could (过去能力): "Fly Guy could say the boy's name"
  • will (将来): "Will Fly Guy survive?"
LT06

Morphology 词形变化

  • -ing: passing, sleeping, going, riding
  • -ed: tumbled, surprised, roared, landed, carried
  • -er 名词: driver, pilot
  • Irregular past: blow→blew, take→took, say→said, see→saw, go→went
  • Compound: motor+boat, air+plane, over+board
LT07

Cohesion / Discourse 语篇衔接

  • Refrain: "Follow that ___!" unifies every scene
  • Meanwhile: 切换场景 (Buzz 追逐 vs Fly Guy 的遭遇)
  • Just then: 突发事件
  • 代词指代: he / him / his → Fly Guy
  • 顺序: 保留链: truck → boat → train → airplane → rocket
LT08

Functional Communication 语用功能

  • Give commands: Follow that car! Stop! Let's go!
  • Retell: First / Then / Next / In the end...
  • Describe: vehicles & their sounds/actions
  • Predict: Will he be safe? Which ride next?
  • Express opinion: Which ride would you take? Why?

4 → "Just then" — "Just then, a rocket roared up from the ground."

5 → car → truck → boat → train → airplane → rocket (start with the car family ride, end at the rocket).

Part F · My Own Chase Story
LT08 · The wind blows your pet out the window! Write your own chase story. Use "Follow that ___!" and at least two prepositions (into, onto, out of, overboard).

Use these sentence starters if you need help:

One day, the wind blew my ___ out the window.
I shouted, "Follow that ___!"
It landed onto the ___.
Then it jumped into the ___.
In the end, I caught it, and it said, "___!"

📋 Answer Key — Model Answer (教师)

Model: "One day, the wind blew my dog out the window. I shouted, 'Follow that bus!' My dog landed onto a car. Then it jumped into an open truck. In the end, I caught it, and it said, 'More rides!'"

Check: story has "Follow that ___!", at least two prepositions (onto, into), and a logical beginning–middle–end.
