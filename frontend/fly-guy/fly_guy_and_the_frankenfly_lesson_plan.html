Overview

故事概览

Story Summary

It was a dark and stormy night. Buzz and Fly Guy were playing — Buzz made puzzles, costumes, and drawings for both of them. Finally, Buzz said, "Time for bed, Fly Guy," and Fly Guy said, "BIZZY!" As Buzz fell asleep, he wondered, "What is Fly Guy making?"

Late that night, a strange light woke Buzz. Fly Guy was making something in his laboratory. Fly Guy turned on the power, and a huge monster — Frankenfly — sat up! Buzz cried, "It's Frankenfly!" The monster walked to the bed and picked Buzz up. But Fly Guy shut off the power, and CRASH! Frankenfly dropped Buzz and fell onto the bed.

Buzz woke up. It was morning. "Wow!" said Buzz. "That was a bad dream." Fly Guy was asleep on the desk. When Buzz asked if Fly Guy had made something, Fly Guy said, "BUZZ!" — and pointed to a painting of Buzz and Fly Guy together! "How did you paint this? My brushes are too big for you." And Buzz smiled: "We are the BEST best friends ever!"

📖 文本特征

  • 3 chapters, ~32 pages, ~287 words
  • 过去时叙述：was/were + -ing 背景 + simple past 事件推进
  • 科幻/怪物词汇：laboratory, power, monster, Frankenfly
  • 拟声词与感叹：BIZZY! / BUZZ! / CRASH!
  • 梦境 vs 现实对照 (dream vs. reality)
  • 核心主题词：friendship, best friends, dream

🎯 教学目标

  • Reading: 理解故事结构（梦境发展），区分"梦"与"现实"
  • Language: 掌握核心词汇、过去时（-ed 与不规则）、-ing 进行时、最高级 best
  • Speaking: 能复述梦境情节、表达对 Frankenfly 的看法
  • Writing: 完成 opinion 写作：Is Fly Guy a good friend?

LT05

Grammar 语法功能

  • Past Continuous: was/were + -ing — "Fly Guy was making something."
  • Simple past: made, said, woke, sat, shut, dropped
  • Question forms: "What is Fly Guy making?" / "Did you make...?"
  • Exclamation: "Wow!" / "It's Frankenfly!" / "CRASH!"
  • Superlative: the BEST best friends
LT06

Morphology 词形变化

  • -ing: play→playing, make→making, paint→painting
  • -ed 规则变化: point→pointed, ask→asked
  • 不规则过去式: make→made, say→said, wake→woke, sit→sat
  • 复合词: Frankenfly = Franken(stein) + fly
  • 最高级: good → better → best
LT07

Cohesion / Discourse 语篇衔接

  • Finally: 时间推进 — "Finally, Buzz said..."
  • Late that night: 时间转换 — 梦境开启
  • As: 伴随 — "As Buzz fell asleep, he wondered..."
  • 代词 he: 指 Buzz / Fly Guy / Frankenfly
  • 因果: 开电→怪物坐起；断电→CRASH
LT08

Functional Communication 语用功能

  • Predict: What is Fly Guy making?
  • Describe: Describe Frankenfly.
  • Retell: 复述暗夜梦境情节
  • Express opinion: Is Fly Guy a good friend? Why?

⏱ 10–20 min · Read Aloud
通读全文 · 理解故事

Chapter 1 精读 (3 min)

页面原文教学重点
p.1–2 "It was a dark and stormy night. Buzz and Fly Guy were playing." 引入句式 "It was a ___ and ___ night"；讲解 stormy；过去进行时 were playing
p.3–6 "Buzz made puzzles / costumes / a drawing for both of them." 反复结构 "made them for both of them"；学习名词 puzzle / costume / drawing
p.7–8 "Finally, Buzz said, 'Time for bed, Fly Guy.' Fly Guy said, BIZZY!" 理解 finally（时间推进）；拟声词 BIZZY! 指向 Fly Guy 很"忙 busy"
p.9 "As Buzz fell asleep he wondered, 'What is Fly Guy making?'" 帮助理解 wonder；设置悬念，训练预测 "What is Fly Guy making?"

Chapter 2 精读 (4 min)

页面原文教学重点
p.10–11 "Late that night a strange light woke up Buzz." 时间转换 late that night；strange light 制造氛围
p.12–13 "Fly Guy was making something in his laboratory." 过去进行时 was making；科学词汇 laboratory
p.14–16 "Fly Guy turned on the power. The monster sat up. Buzz cried, 'It's Frankenfly!'" 动词 chain：turned on → sat up → cried；核心词 power, monster, Frankenfly
p.17–20 "He walked to the bed. Frankenfly picked up Buzz. Fly Guy yelled." 理解 He=Frankenfly；动作连续（walked / picked up / yelled）制造紧张
p.21–22 "Fly Guy shut off the power. CRASH! Frankenfly dropped Buzz..." 因果：shut off the power → CRASH；拟声词 CRASH!

Chapter 3 精读 (3 min)

页面原文教学重点
p.23–24 "Buzz fell out of bed and woke up. It was morning. 'Wow! That was a bad dream.'" 梦醒转折：确认这一切是 dream；对比梦境与现实
p.25–27 "'Fly Guy, did you make something last night?' 'BUZZ!' 'You made me?' 'YEZZZ!'" 一般过去疑问 did you make；Fly Guy 的趣味回答 BUZZ! / YEZZZ!
p.28–30 "He pointed to a piece of paper. 'It's a painting of you and me!' 'How did you paint this?'" 理解 painting；"How did you paint this? My brushes are too big for you." 巧妙
p.31–32 "Buzz said, 'We are the BEST best friends ever!'" 主题点题：最高级 best；理解全书"友谊"主题
💡 朗读提示 在黑夜里朗读第 2 章可适当放慢、压低声音制造悬念；到 CRASH! 时突然加大音量，增强戏剧效果并呼应拟声词。

⏱ 5–18 min · LT05 Grammar
语法功能深度分析

① Past Continuous 过去进行时 — 背景 (5 min)

原文句功能结构
"Buzz and Fly Guy were playing."当时正在进行的背景was/were + 动词-ing
"Fly Guy was making something."当时正在发生的动作was + 动词-ing
"Buzz was wondering..."（转述意）心理状态的动作化was + 动词-ing

类比：过去进行时就像"照片里的那个瞬间"，常常搭配 simple past 打断。
"Fly Guy was making something when a strange light woke Buzz."（教师示范）

③ Question Forms 疑问句 (2 min)

Present Continuous: What + is + subject + -ing?
"What is Fly Guy making?"
Simple Past: Did + subject + base verb?
"Did you make something last night?"

④ Superlative + Exclamation 最高级与感叹 (2 min)

the + best / the most + adj
good → better → best — "the BEST best friends ever!"

大写 BEST + best 叠加 = maximum 强调。感叹词 WOW! / BUZZ! / CRASH! 表达强烈情绪。

I think Fly Guy is a very good friend. First, he stayed awake all night to make a surprise for Buzz. Also, the surprise was a beautiful painting of Buzz and Fly Guy together, which shows he cares. In conclusion, Fly Guy is a good friend because he always thinks of Buzz.
