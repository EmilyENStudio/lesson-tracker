Overview

故事概览

Story Summary

Blessed with a pet fly named Fly Guy who can say his name — "BUZZ!" — Buzz plays with him, builds him a glass house, and feeds him. Fly Guy's favorite food is brown, oozy, lumpy, and smelly. One day Fly Guy goes flying all by himself, and when he comes home, Buzz is gone. Fly Guy is hungry, so off he flies to find something to eat.

Hungry as ever, Fly Guy keeps spotting food that "isn't oozy, lumpy, or smelly — but close enough!" He tries to land on a hamburger, a pizza, a pile of dog bones, and even a bird's roadkill — only to be chased away each time with a firm "Shoo, fly!" By nightfall he is very hungry, very tired, and very lost. Just as all hope seems gone, he spots something brown, oozy, lumpy, and smelly. "You found our picnic!" cries Buzz. "And here is your favorite Shoo Fly Pie!" Fly Guy is very happy — home, food, and Buzz, all in one place.

📖 文本特征

  • 3 chapters, ~30 pages
  • 重复句式：It wasn't ___, ___, or ___. But it was ___. Close enough!
  • 关键修辞手法：反复 (repetition)、渐进句 (sequence)、双关 (Shoo Fly Pie)
  • 核心主题词：hungry, lost, food, home, favorite

🎯 教学目标

  • Reading: 理解"找食物-被赶走-意外团聚"的故事循环，识别反复句式
  • Language: 掌握感官形容词 (oozy/lumpy/smelly)、过去时、描述食物句型
  • Speaking: 能按顺序复述、用形容词描述食物、表达喜好
  • Writing: 完成 food description / opinion 写作
Language Target Framework

LT 框架总览 — Shoo, Fly Guy!

以下 8 个模块构成本书的完整语言目标体系，两个课时分工推进。

LT01

Vocabulary 词汇

12 个核心词，含 IPA、词性、搭配、例句、词族。

  • shoo, fly, hungry, tired, lost
  • oozy, lumpy, smelly, favorite
  • shout, enough, picnic
LT02

Phonics / Decoding 语音拼读

  • sh- digraph: shoo, shout, shouted (书名核心!)
  • -y 结尾形容词: hungry, oozy, lumpy, smelly, happy
  • br-/gr-/fl-/sq- blends: brown, growl, flew, squawk
  • -ed 过去式词尾: shouted, yelled, growled, squawked
  • oo 元音组合: oozy, food
LT03

Pronunciation 发音

  • 音节切分：hun-gry (2), oo-zy (2), fa-vor-ite (3), pic-nic (2), e-nough (2)
  • -ed 词尾读音：/d/ growled · /t/ squawked · /ɪd/ shouted
  • -y 结尾读 /i/：happy, hungry, smelly, silly
  • 句惯用语：Close enough! 连读与重音
LT04

Sentence Patterns 句型

  • It wasn't ___, ___, or ___. But it was ___.
  • Fly Guy flew on until he saw something else.
  • A [animal/person] [report verb]-ed, "___."
  • Shoo, fly! (祈使句)
  • That is my ___. (展示/声明归属)
LT05

Grammar 语法功能

  • Simple past: flew, went, looked, saw, found, said
  • be + adj 描述: It wasn't... But it was...
  • Possession/my: my hamburger, my pizza, my bones
  • Result (so): "So off he flew."
  • Could (wonder): "Could it be? Yes!"
LT06

Morphology 词形变化

  • 名词→-y 形容词: ooze→oozy, lump→lumpy, smell→smelly
  • -ed 过去式: shout→shouted, yell→yelled, cry→cried (y→ied)
  • 不规则过去: see→saw, find→found, fly→flew, go→went
  • 复合词: road + kill = roadkill
  • -ing: flying, looking
LT07

Cohesion / Discourse 语篇衔接

  • until (顺序): "flew on until he saw..."
  • but (转折): "But it was brown. Close enough!" ×4
  • so (结果): "So off he flew."
  • and (并列/递进): "And here is your favorite Shoo Fly Pie!"
  • 代词 it (指代食物) · 惯用语 "Close enough!"
LT08

Functional Communication 语用功能

  • Describe: Describe Fly Guy's favorite food. What is it like?
  • Retell: What did Fly Guy try first / next / at last?
  • Sequencing: First... Then... Next... At last...
  • Express preference: What is your favorite food? Why?
  • Role-play: "Shoo, fly!" claims & protests

⏱ 10–20 min · Read Aloud
通读全文 · 理解故事

Chapter 1 精读 (4 min)

页面原文教学重点
p.1–3 "A boy had a pet fly. The boy called his pet Fly Guy. Fly Guy could say the boy's name — BUZZ!" 复习第一本书的核心设定；"could" 表示过去的能力
p.4–7 "Buzz played with Fly Guy. Buzz made him a glass house. Best of all, Buzz fed him." 预算结构 "Best of all..."；理解 Buzz 对 Fly Guy 有多好
p.8 "Fly Guy's favorite food was brown, oozy, lumpy, and smelly." 引出 4 个感官形容词；favorite 的概念
p.9–10 "One day Fly Guy went flying by himself. When he came home, Buzz was gone." 情节转折：Buzz 不见了！Fly Guy 会怎么办？
p.11 "Fly Guy was hungry. So off he flew." "So off he flew" 的倒装表达；hungry 引出动机

Chapter 2 精读 (4 min)

页面原文教学重点
p.12–14 "It wasn't oozy, lumpy, or smelly. But it was brown. Close enough!" / "That is my hamburger!" 引出核心句式型；"my" 归属表达；多次"被赶走"的第一幕
p.15–17 "It wasn't brown, lumpy, or smelly. But it was oozy. Close enough!" / "That is my pizza!" / "Shoo, fly!" "Shoo, fly!" 首次出现；对比 was/wasn't
p.18–21 "It wasn't brown, oozy, or smelly. But it was lumpy. Close enough!" / "Those are my bones." 反复句式续；动物插话 (growled)
p.22–24 "It wasn't brown, oozy, or lumpy. But it was smelly. Close enough!" / "That is my roadkill." / "Shoo, fly!" 四次重复的完整呈现；讨论：为什么每次都"Close enough"？

Chapter 3 精读 (4 min)

页面原文教学重点
p.25–27 "Fly Guy was very hungry. And he was very tired. He looked around. Fly Guy was very lost." 情绪升级：hungry → tired → lost；重复 "very" 强调
p.28 "Fly Guy saw something. Could it be? Yes! It was brown, oozy, lumpy, and smelly." 悬念与转折；四个形容词全到齐 = 完美食物!
p.29–30 "'You found our picnic!' cried Buzz. 'And here is your favorite Shoo Fly Pie!' Fly Guy was very happy!" 圆满结局：Buzz 一直都在野餐！双关 "Shoo Fly Pie"
⏱ 20–33 min · LT01 Vocabulary
核心词汇深度讲解（12 词）
📋 词汇教学原则 每个词从 Recognize → Understand → Use 三个层次推进。先让学生听/看到词，再理解含义和用法，最后尝试产出。
shoo /ʃuː/
verb (叹词/动词)
to wave something away and tell it to go away
"Shoo, fly!" → The farmer shooed the chickens out of the yard. (书名双关)
fly /flaɪ/
noun / verb
an insect with wings; to move through the air
A boy had a pet fly. → Fly Guy flew on until he saw something. (noun vs. verb)
hungry /ˈhʌŋɡri/
adjective noun: hunger · -er: hungrier
wanting to eat food
Fly Guy was very hungry. → Are you hungry? Let's get a snack.
tired /ˈtaɪərd/
adjective
needing rest or sleep
He was very tired after flying all day. → We were tired after the long walk.
lost /lɔːst/
adjective
unable to find your way; not knowing where you are
Fly Guy was very lost. → Help! I'm lost. I can't find the park.
oozy /ˈuːzi/
adjective noun: ooze
thick and dripping slowly, like soft mud
It wasn't oozy... But it was oozy. Close enough! → The cake was soft and oozy.
lumpy /ˈlʌmpi/
adjective
full of lumps; not smooth or even
It wasn't lumpy... But it was lumpy. → The mashed potatoes were a little lumpy.
smelly /ˈsmeli/
adjective verb: smell
having a strong (usually unpleasant) smell
It was smelly — his favorite! → The garbage is smelly. Yuck!
favorite /ˈfeɪvərɪt/
adjective / noun
liked better than anything else
Fly Guy's favorite food was... → Here is your favorite Shoo Fly Pie!
shout /ʃaʊt/
verb 同义: yell · 神情: growl, squawk
to say something very loudly
A boy shouted, "That is my hamburger!" → Don't shout; I can hear you.
enough /ɪˈnʌf/
adverb / determiner
as much or as many as is needed
"But it was brown. Close enough!" → Do we have enough food for the picnic?
picnic /ˈpɪknɪk/
noun
an outdoor meal, often with friends or family
"You found our picnic!" cried Buzz. → We had a picnic in the park.

⏱ 33–43 min · LT02 Phonics + LT03 Pronunciation
语音拼读与发音训练

Phonics Focus (5 min)

从本书中提取高频语音模式，引导学生发现规律：

sh- 二合字母 Digraph（书名核心）

sh- shoo, shout, shouted

"Shoo, fly!" 是全书最常出现的句子。sh- 发 /ʃ/，双唇前伸，像让大家"嘘"。

-y 结尾形容词（全书高频）

-y hungry, oozy, lumpy, smelly, happy, very

-y 结尾常读 /i/。这些形容食物的词在书中反复出现，是理解故事的关键。

辅音连缀 Consonant Blends

br- brown gr- growled fl- flew, flying sq- squawked

oo 元音组合 & -ed 词尾

oo /uː/ — oozy, food -ed shouted · yelled · growled · squawked

-ed 有三个读音：/ɪd/ shouted, /d/ yelled·growled, /t/ squawked。

⏱ 43–50 min · LT04 Sentence Patterns + Wrap-up
核心句型初探 + 小结

引入本课核心句式，为第二课时深度语法做铺垫：

It wasn't ___, ___, or ___. But it was ___. Close enough!
"It wasn't oozy, lumpy, or smelly. But it was brown. Close enough!"

📌 全书最核心的反复句式，出现 4 次 — 每次强调一个缺失的感官要素。

Fly Guy flew on until he saw something else.
"Fly Guy flew on until he saw something else." (重复 3 次连接场景)

📌 until 连接"动作延续"与"新发现"，是推动情节顺序的关键。

A [person/animal] [report verb]-ed, "___."
A boy shouted, "That is my hamburger!" · A dog growled, "Those are my bones!"

📌 引述动词 (said, shouted, yelled, growled, squawked, cried) 让对话更生动。

Shoo, [noun]! / That is my ___!
"Shoo, fly!" · "That is my pizza!"

📌 祈使句 (Shoo!) 表达命令/驱赶；所有格 (my) 表达拥有/占有。

📝 第一课时小结 回顾 Big Question: "Will Fly Guy find something to eat?" 让学生用一句式作答：
"Fly Guy tried to eat ___, but ___ said, 'Shoo, fly!'"
（为第二课时的 retelling 做铺垫。）

LT07 · Cohesion 语篇衔接 (4 min)

⏱ 26–40 min · LT08 Functional Communication
口语输出与写作任务

Activity 1: Retell the Story (5 min)

学生两人一组，用以下框架复述故事：

Story Retelling Frame:

First, Fly Guy went flying and found that Buzz was gone.
Then, he got hungry and flew off to find something to eat.
Next, he tried a hamburger, a pizza, some bones, and even roadkill — but everyone said, "Shoo, fly!"
After that, he got very hungry, very tired, and very lost.
In the end, he found Buzz's picnic — and his favorite Shoo Fly Pie!

Activity 2: Describe Your Favorite Food (5 min)

Speaking Prompts:

  • "What is your favorite food? Describe it."
  • "Is it brown / oozy / lumpy / smelly / sweet?"
  • "Would you say 'Close enough!' about anything you eat? What?"

Language scaffold:

"My favorite food is ___.
It is ___, ___, and ___.
It is not ___, but it is ___. Close enough!"

Activity 3: Writing — Food Description / Opinion (4 min 指导 + 课后完成)

Writing Prompt:

Describe your favorite food. Use at least three describing words. At the end, write one sentence with "Close enough!"

Writing Frame (scaffold):

My favorite food is ___.
It is ___, ___, and ___.
It is not ___, but it is ___.
Close enough! It is ___. (finish)
