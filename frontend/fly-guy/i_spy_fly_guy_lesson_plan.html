Overview

故事概览

Story Summary

Buzz has a pet fly named Fly Guy, and Fly Guy can say the boy's name. One day Buzz and Fly Guy go outside to play hide-and-seek. Fly Guy hides in the garbage can — he always hides there, so he can eat while Buzz looks for him. "I spy Fly Guy!" says Buzz, and then it's Buzz's turn to hide. Buzz hides in the garden shed and shuts the door, but clever Fly Guy finds a way in. "You are GOOD!" says Buzz. "It's your turn to hide again."

Fly Guy hides in the garbage can once more — but just then the garbageman comes, dumps the garbage into his truck, and drives away! "Follow that truck!" cries Buzz. Dad races him all the way to the town dump. Buzz cries out, "Fly Guy, where are you? Answer me!" A zillion flies buzz back "ZZZZZ!" Buzz spied one fly hiding, one eating, one landing on his hand — "Do I spy Fly Guy?" — but each one just flies away, boinks him, or bites him. Buzz is heartbroken. Was Fly Guy gone forever?

Then Buzz remembers — they are still playing a game! "Okay, Fly Guy, I give up. You win." Suddenly he hears a voice from above, and cries out "I SPY FLY GUY!" — because Fly Guy had been hiding there all along, waiting for his boy to find him. Best friends, reunited.

📖 文本特征

  • 3 chapters, ~30 pages
  • 反复句式：I spy ___ / Do I spy ___ ?
  • 地点介词：in the garbage can, in the shed, on his hand, from above
  • 核心主题词：hide-and-seek, playing a game, friendship, cleverness

🎯 教学目标

  • Reading: 理解游戏规则与悬念结构（捉迷藏怎会变成找苍蝇？）
  • Language: 掌握核心词汇、地点介词、过去时态、I spy 句式
  • Speaking: 能玩 I Spy 游戏、复述故事、回答 Where / How 问题
  • Writing: 完成 opinion / retell 写作：I spy + 位置描述
Language Target Framework

LT 框架总览 — I Spy Fly Guy!

以下 8 个模块构成本书的完整语言目标体系，两个课时分工推进。

LT01

Vocabulary 词汇

12 个核心词，含 IPA、词性、搭配、例句、词族。

  • spy, hide, seek, found
  • garbage can, shed, garbageman, dump
  • zillion, follow, answer, boink, bite
LT02

Phonics / Decoding 语音拼读

  • sp- blend: spy, spied, spying
  • tr- blend: truck, trash
  • dr- blend: drove, drove, drove
  • -mp final: dump, stomp
  • -ck: truck, kick, back
  • 拟声词 Z /z/: zillion, ZZZZZ
LT03

Pronunciation 发音

  • 音节切分：spy (1), gar-bage (2), gar-bage-man (3), gizilion / zi-llion (2)
  • 重音：GAR-bage, REM-ber, a-BOVE, to-GE-ther
  • 连读：spied_a fly, hid in_the
  • /z/ 音拖长：ZZZZZ!（拟声）
LT04

Sentence Patterns 句型

  • I spy [noun]! / Do I spy [noun]?
  • It's my / your turn to [verb].
  • Let's play [game].
  • ___ hid in / on / from [place].
  • Follow that truck!
LT05

Grammar 语法功能

  • Ability: can / could — "Fly Guy could say the boy's name."
  • Simple past: hid, came, dumped, drove, cried, spied, flew, bit
  • Prepositions of place: in / on / from / into / above
  • Questions: "Where are you?" "How will I find Fly Guy?"
  • Imperatives: "Follow that truck!" "Answer me!"
LT06

Morphology 词形变化

  • Irregular past: hide→hid, come→came, drive→drove, fly→flew, bite→bit, hear→heard, give→gave, say→said
  • -ed: dumped, kicked, remembered
  • y→i-ed: spy→spied, cry→cried
  • -ing: hide→hiding, play→playing, eat→eating
  • Compound: garbage+man, hide+and+seek
LT07

Cohesion / Discourse 语篇衔接

  • Sequence: One day → Just then → all the way → Then
  • and: "drove and drove and drove"（反复并列）
  • again: 重复动作推进节奏
  • 代词 he: "Fly Guy hid... He always hid..." He = Fly Guy
  • 对话标记词：said, cried, yelled, asked
LT08

Functional Communication 语用功能

  • Play a game: 玩"I Spy"游戏 — "I spy something..."
  • Describe position: Where did Fly Guy hide? On / In...
  • Retell: What happened first / next / at the end?
  • Answer questions: "Where are you?" "Answer me!"
  • Express opinion: Where would you hide? Why?

⏱ 10–20 min · Read Aloud
通读全文 · 理解故事

Chapter 1 精读 (5 min)

页面原文教学重点
开场 "A boy had a pet fly. He named him Fly Guy. Fly Guy could say the boy's name." 引入 could（过去能力）与命名；复习系列老梗 "BUZZ!"
Ch.1 a "Let's play hide-and-seek," said Buzz. "Fly Guy hid in the garbage can. He always hid in the garbage can. He liked to eat while Buzz looked for him." 理解 always（习惯）与 in the garbage can（地点）；Fly Guy 是有计划的小机灵
Ch.1 b "I spy Fly Guy!" said Buzz. "It's my turn to hide." 核心句式 "I spy ___!" 与 "It's my turn to ___."
Ch.1 c "Buzz hid in the garden shed and shut the door. Fly Guy found a way in. 'You are GOOD!' said Buzz." 地点 in the garden shed；学习 found（find 的过去式）；Fly Guy 太聪明

Chapter 2 精读 (5 min)

页面原文教学重点
Ch.2 a "Just then, the garbageman came. He dumped the garbage into the truck and drove away." "just then"（就在这时）制造转折；garbageman + dump
Ch.2 b "Follow that truck!" cried Buzz. "The truck drove and drove and drove, all the way to the town dump." 命令句 "Follow that truck!"；"drove and drove and drove" 反复结构强调路程远
Ch.2 c "Fly Guy, where are you? Answer me! A zillion flies all answered, ZZZZZ!" 特殊疑问句 "where are you?"；zillion = 无数；拟声词 ZZZZZ!
Ch.2 d "They all can say my name! How will I find Fly Guy?" Buzz spied a fly hiding... eating... landing on his hand. "How will I find...?" 疑问；重复 "Do I spy Fly Guy?" 三连；每个答案都否

Chapter 3 精读 (5 min)

页面原文教学重点
Ch.3 a "Buzz was sad. Was Fly Guy gone forever? He kicked a can. He kicked a jar." 疑问句 "Was Fly Guy gone forever?"；forever；情感转折
Ch.3 b "Then Buzz remembered. They were still playing a game. 'Okay, Fly Guy, I give up. You win.'" then（恍然大悟）；"I give up. You win."（认输也是赢）
Ch.3 c "He heard a voice from above. 'I SPY FLY GUY!' cried Buzz. And Fly Guy said..." from above（上方）；情感高潮！感人重逢；结尾悬念
💡 阅读策略 全书用"找苍蝇"作为悬念主线，每次 "Do I spy Fly Guy?" 都指向错误的苍蝇，最后 "I SPY FLY GUY!" 反转。请学生带着"这次是真的吗？"读下去。

① can / could — 表达能力 (5 min)

原文句功能结构
"Fly Guy could say the boy's name."过去能力（肯定）主语 + could + 动词原形

Activity 2: Describe & Retell (5 min)

Discussion Prompts:

  • "Where did Fly Guy hide at the start?" → In the garbage can.
  • "Why was Buzz sad in the dump?" → He thought Fly Guy was gone forever.
  • "Where was Fly Guy at the end?" → From above.
  • "Is it a good hiding place? Would you hide there?"

Retell frame:

First, they played hide-and-seek.
Then, the garbageman took the can away.
Next, Buzz followed the truck to the dump.
At the end, Buzz found Fly Guy hiding above. "I spy Fly Guy!"

Activity 3: Writing — Opinion / Retell (4 min 指导 + 课后完成)

Part F · Writing — I Spy Me!
LT08 · Where would you hide in hide-and-seek? Write about it. Use at least 4 sentences.

Use these sentence starters if you need help:

I would hide in / under / behind ___.
Buzz would spy me there.
"I spy ___!" said Buzz.
Then it's my turn to ___. I win!

✅ 参考答案 · Model Answer (Part F)

Sample (suggested, not the only answer):

"I would hide under my bed. Buzz would walk in and look around. He would say, 'Where is she?' I am quiet. Then Buzz would laugh and say, 'I spy you under the bed!' It is my turn to hide again. I win!"

（评分关注：是否使用位置介词 on/in/under，是否使用 "I spy" 句式，句子是否完整、流畅。）
