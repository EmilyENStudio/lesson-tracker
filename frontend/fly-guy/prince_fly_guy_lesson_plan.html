Overview

故事概览

Story Summary

A boy named Buzz has homework to do — he has to write a fairy tale. He asks his pet fly, Fly Guy, for help. "How does this sound? Once upon a time..." Buzz brainstorms out loud, inventing and revising his story on the spot: an ugly troll? a smelly pig herder? No! What about a handsome prince? He walks — or rides — or even flies to a dark castle. He eats cold porridge. What if he kissed a frog? — "I've got it! He rescued a beautiful princess." But a giant lived in the castle. The giant chased them and knocked them to the ground.

The princess threw her crown, and it hit the giant on the nose. The giant fell down and ran away. The prince and princess flew home, made matching crowns, and lived happily ever after. "The end," says Buzz. "I like my fairy tale. Hey, want to write another one?" And then he begins again: "Once there was a hairy dwarf..." — proving that Buzz and Fly Guy are a wonderful storytelling team.

📖 文本特征

  • 3 chapters, 童话故事创作过程完整呈现
  • 连续 brainstorm 句式：What about...? / What if...? / How does this sound?
  • 童话框架词：Once upon a time ... happily ever after ... The end.
  • 修饰语序列：ugly troll / smelly pig herder / handsome prince / dark castle / beautiful princess / hairy dwarf
  • 核心主题词：imagination, collaboration, creative writing, revision

🎯 教学目标

  • Reading: 理解"边编边想"的创作过程，梳理情节的生成与修订（idea → reject → revise → keep）
  • Language: 掌握童话叙述过去时、what if 假设句、Once upon a time 框架、修饰语排序
  • Speaking: 能提出点子、赞同/否定、复述童话
  • Writing: 用给定框架创作并修订自己的小童话
Language Target Framework

LT 框架总览 — Prince Fly Guy

以下 8 个模块构成本书的完整语言目标体系，两个课时分工推进。

LT01

Vocabulary 词汇

12 个核心词，含 IPA、词性、搭配、例句、词族。

  • fairy tale, ugly, troll, smelly, herder
  • handsome, prince, castle, princess, giant
  • crown, rescue
LT02

Phonics / Decoding 语音拼读

  • tr- blend: troll, tricky
  • cr- blend: crown, cried? (no) — crown, crouched? → crown, crawl?
  • dw- blend: dwarf
  • wr- silent w: wrote, write
  • soft c (/s/): once, prince, princess
  • soft g (/dʒ/): giant
  • magic-e 长元音: rode, made
LT03

Pronunciation 发音

  • 音节切分：cas-tle (2), prin-cess (2), hand-some (2), gi-ant (2)
  • 重音模式：prinCESS, HAN-some, GE... GI-ant, RES-cue
  • 弱读: was /wəz/, to /tə/
  • 连读: rode_to, fell_down, knocked_them
LT04

Sentence Patterns 句型

  • Once upon a time, there was a [adj] [noun].
  • What about a [adj] [noun]? — 提出新点子
  • What if [subject] [verb]-ed ...? — 假设
  • How does this sound? — 征询意见
  • The [adj] [prince] [verb]-ed to the [adj] [castle].
LT05

Grammar 语法功能

  • Fairy-tale narrative past: there was, walked, flew, rescued, lived
  • be 动词过去式 was: "Once upon a time, there was..."
  • What if + past 假设句: "What if he rode...?"
  • 过去能力 could: "Fly Guy could say the boy's name."
  • 目的/请求: Can you help me? / I have to write...
LT06

Morphology 词形变化

  • -y 形容词后缀: smell → smelly, hair → hairy
  • -ly 副词: happy → happily
  • 分词形容词: match → matching (crowns)
  • 不规则过去式: is→was, ride→rode, fly→flew, throw→threw, fall→fell, run→ran, make→made, hit→hit
  • 规则 -ed: rescue→rescued, chase→chased, kiss→kissed
LT07

Cohesion / Discourse 语篇衔接

  • 童话框架词: Once upon a time ... happily ever after ... The end.
  • 递进连接词: then, after that, but
  • 情节推进: What about → What if → "I've got it!" 链式创作
  • 代词指代: he / the prince / the princess / the giant
  • 结尾衔接: "Want to write another one?" → "Once there was..."
LT08

Functional Communication 语用功能

  • Create: invent and revise a fairy tale
  • Suggest: What about...? What if...? How about...?
  • Agree / disagree: Okay. No! I've got it!
  • Retell: 用童话框架复述故事
  • Give opinion: Which character do you like best? Why?

通读全文 · 理解创作过程

Chapter 1–2 精读 (5 min)

页面原文教学重点
开口 "A boy had a pet fly. He named him Fly Guy. And Fly Guy could say the boy's name — BUZZ!" 复习系列背景；could + verb 过去能力
p.1–2 "I have homework to do. I have to write a fairy tale. Can you help me, Fly Guy?" 理解 have to（必须）；引入 fairy tale
p.3–4 "How does this sound? Once upon a time..." "Once upon a time, there was an ugly troll." 童话开场：Once upon a time, there was + adj + noun
p.5–6 "You don't like that? Well, what about a smelly pig herder?" "No? What about a handsome prince?" 互动句式 What about + a + adj + noun?；为什么换角色？
p.7–8 "The handsome prince walked to the dark castle." "Maybe instead of walking... what if he rode...?" "He flew to the dark castle!" walked / rode / flew 交通工具变化；What if...? 假设

Chapter 3 精读 (5 min)

页面原文教学重点
p.9–10 "the handsome prince ate cold porridge." "What if he kissed a frog?" "I've got it! He rescued a beautiful princess." "But a giant lived in the dark castle." ate / kissed / rescued 过去式；but 转折引来冲突（giant）
p.11–12 "The giant chased the handsome prince and the beautiful princess." "He knocked them down to the ground." 理解 chase / knock down 动词链；冲突升级
p.13–14 "The princess threw her crown." "It hit the giant on the nose." "The giant fell down." "He ran away." threw / hit / fell / ran 一串不规则过去式；crown 是解围关键道具
p.15–16 "The prince and the princess flew home." "They made matching crowns." "And they lived happily ever after." "The end." 童话收尾：happily ever after 与 The end.
末尾 "I like my fairy tale. Hey, want to write another one?" "Okay. Once there was a hairy dwarf..." 结尾衔接：故事再次开始 → 创作是可循环的
💡 阅读提示 引导学生注意"角色被否 → 换新角色"的过程（troll/herder 被否，prince 被保留）。这不是浪费，而是创作中的修订 (revision)。

引导学生发现童话词族与词形变化：

变化ExampleRule
verb → adj (-y)smell → smelly, hair → hairy名词/动词 + y = 形容词

LT07 · Cohesion 语篇衔接 (4 min)

童话靠"框架词"和"连接词"把一个个点子串成完整故事：

DeviceFunctionExample from Text
Once upon a time 故事开头标记 "Once upon a time, there was an ugly troll."
but 转折，引入冲突 "But a giant lived in the dark castle."
递进创作杠 What about → What if → "I've got it!" 推动情节生成与修订 "What about a handsome prince?" → "What if he flew...?" → "I've got it! He rescued a princess."
happily ever after / The end 故事结尾标记 "And they lived happily ever after." "The end," said Buzz.
代词 he 指代 — 需看上下文 "He rescued a princess." → He = the prince
"He chased them." → He = the giant
💡 讨论：童话的"书挡"bookends 一个童话往往以"Once upon a time"开始，以"happily ever after"和"The end"收尾。让学生找找本册的开头与结尾，体会框架的力量。

✅ Answer Key (Sample answer)

Once upon a time, there was a little dragon who could not fly. But then a giant rabbit stole his golden egg. What if he asked the princess for help? I've got it! The princess made him a sparkly scarf, and he floated down from the mountain. In the end, he got his egg back and learned to fly. And they lived happily ever after. The end.

(Accept any complete fairy tale that opens with "Once upon a time," includes a problem and a solution, and ends with "happily ever after." Check for past tense and capital letters / full stops.)
