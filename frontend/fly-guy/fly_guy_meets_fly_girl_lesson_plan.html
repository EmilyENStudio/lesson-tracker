Overview

故事概览

Story Summary

Buzz and Fly Guy are bored, so they go for a walk, play chase, and cool off in the fountain. Suddenly they see a girl running — with a fly chasing her! Buzz says, "Don't worry. Flies aren't pests. They are pets." The girl, Liz, introduces her pet: "This is my pet. Her name is Fly Girl."

Buzz introduces Fly Guy, and the two pets start to compare. "Fly Guy can do tricks." — "Fly Girl can do tricks, too!" "Fly Guy eats gross stuff." — "Fly Girl eats grosser stuff!" "Fly Guy can say my name." — "Fly Girl can say my name, too!" Then Buzz and Liz play on the swings while the two flies sit side by side. Fly Guy and Fly Girl talk and talk — "That is fly talk for 'You are nice, too.'" — and at last, "That is fly talk for 'Let's be friends.'" They say "See you soon," and Buzz smiles: "That was fun!" Fly Guy has a new friend!

📖 文本特征 / Story Features

  • 3 chapters, ~32 pages
  • 关键句型：___ can __, too! / That is ___ for "___"
  • 对比手法：gross vs. grosser；pests vs. pets
  • 核心主题：friendship, meeting new friends, comparing abilities

🎯 教学目标 / Objectives

  • Reading: 理解三章故事结构，提取关键细节
  • Language: 掌握核心词汇、can + too、比较级 -er、aren't/are
  • Speaking: 能介绍朋友、比较能力、表达交友意向
  • Writing: 完成交友/比较主题写作（Meet a New Friend）
Language Target Framework

LT 框架总览 — Fly Guy Meets Fly Girl

LT01

Vocabulary 词汇

12 个核心词，含 IPA、词性、搭配、例句、词族。

  • meet, bored, chase, fountain, pest
  • gross, trick, swings, nice, friend

LT02

Phonics / Decoding 语音拼读

  • fl- blend: fly, flew
  • gr- blend: gross, grosser
  • sw- blend: swings, swing
  • ch- digraph: chase, chasing
  • ea- vowel team: eat, eats
  • Long i (CVCe/-y): side, nice, my, fly
  • Comparative -er: gross → grosser
LT03

Pronunciation 发音

  • 音节切分：foun-tain (2), run-ning (2), chased (1), talk-ed (2)
  • 重音模式：FOUNtain, beSIDE, BOred
  • 双元音 /aɪ/: my, side, nice, fly
  • /oʊ/（长 o）: gross, both, go, hold
  • -er 结尾弱音 /ər/: grosser, another
LT04

Sentence Patterns 句型

  • ___ can [verb]. → ___ can [verb], too!
  • This is my pet. Her name is ___. (介绍)
  • ___ eats gross(er) stuff. (比较)
  • That is ___ for "___". (定义/解释)
  • Flies aren't pests. They are pets. (否定+分类)
  • Let's ___. (提议：Let's do something fun / be friends)
LT05

Grammar 语法功能

  • Ability: can + verb — "He can do tricks."
  • too (也): 句末附加 — "Fly Girl can do tricks, too!"
  • Comparative -er: gross → grosser
  • be + negative plural: aren't / are — "Flies aren't pests."
  • be + adjective: "You are nice."
  • Let's + verb: suggestion
LT06

Morphology 词形变化

  • -er comparative: gross → grosser
  • -ing: run→running, chase→chasing, talk→talking
  • -ed: play→played, cool→cooled, walk→walked
  • plural -s: pet→pets, pest→pests, trick→tricks, swing→swings
  • Irregular past: go→went, say→said, meet→met, sit→sat
  • Compound/idiom: side by side; new + friend
LT07

Cohesion / Discourse 语篇衔接

  • too (附加): 表示"同样"
  • both: "Fly Guy and Fly Girl both said..."
  • and / then: 并列与时间顺序
  • 代词指代: her (Fly Girl), he (Fly Guy)
  • 定义式衔接: That is fly talk for "..."
  • 结尾呼应: "Fly Guy has a new friend!"
LT08

Functional Communication 语用功能

  • Meet & introduce: Introduce your pet / yourself
  • Compare: Who can do what? gross vs. grosser
  • Make friends: greet, be nice, "Let's be friends"
  • Retell: What happened first / next / at the end?
  • Describe: Describe Liz & Fly Girl
  • Express opinion: Would you like a pet fly? Why?

Lesson 2: What Makes a Friend?

Focus: Review → Grammar → Morphology/Cohesion → Functional Communication → Wrap-up
⏱ 0–5 min · Review
回顾第一课
  • 快速复述：Fly Guy 和 Fly Girl 在哪里、如何相遇？
  • 抢答："What can Fly Guy do?" → He can do tricks / say the name.
  • 复习 can + too：完成 "Fly Girl can say my name, ___!" → too
⏱ 5–18 min · LT05 Grammar
语法功能分析

1) 能力表达 can + verb

表示"能/会做某事"：can + 动词原形。

  • "He can do tricks."（会做把戏）
  • "Fly Girl can say my name."（会说名字）

2) too（也）— 句末

在肯定句末尾加 too 表示"同样"。这是本书反复出现的呼应结构。

  • "Fly Guy can do tricks." → "Fly Girl can do tricks, too!"

3) 比较级 -er（形容词变化）

单音节形容词加 -er 表示"更…"：

  • gross → grosser（"Fly Girl eats grosser stuff!"）

4) be 动词复数否定 aren't / 肯定 are

  • "Flies aren't pests. They are pets."（否定 + 肯定对比）

5) Let's + 动作（提议）

  • "Let's do something fun." / "Let's be friends."
💡 教学提示 用黑板左右两列做 "can / can't / too" 的口头替换操练，每人说一句关于 Fly Guy 或 Fly Girl 的话。

LT07 · 语篇衔接 Cohesion

  • too（附加/呼应）："…can do tricks, too!"
  • both（两者都）："Fly Guy and Fly Girl both said..."
  • and / then（并列&时序）："talked and talked" / "Then Fly Guy said..."
  • 代词指代：her（Fly Girl）、he（Fly Guy）—— "Her name is Fly Girl."
  • 定义式衔接："That is fly talk for..."（解释上句的"苍蝇语"）
  • 结尾呼应："Fly Guy has a new friend!"（点题）
🎯 引导提问 In "A fly was chasing her," who does "her" mean? How do you know?
⏱ 26–40 min · LT08 Functional Communication
交际任务 · 口语/写作输出

Task 1 · Meet & Introduce (7 min)

学生两人一组，扮演 Buzz/Liz，用下列句型介绍自己的"宠物"（可用自己的名字/同学名）：

  • "This is my pet. His/Her name is ___."
  • "He/She can ___." / "He/She can ___, too!"

Task 2 · Trick-Off 能力比拼 (7 min)

基于书中能力比较，做口头 Mini-Contest：每个学生说一件自家宠物/自己"更厉害"的能力，用比较级或 too 结构。

🔊 语言支架 / Sentence Frames:
"Mine can ___."  ·  "Mine can ___, too!"  ·  "___ eats grosser stuff!"  ·  "Both of us can ___."

Task 3 · Make-a-Friend Dialogue (7 min)

用下列句子编一小段"交朋友"对话（2-3 轮），然后表演：

  • Hello. My name is ___. / You are nice. / That is ___ for "___".
  • Do you want to ___? → Sure! / Let's be friends! / See you soon!
⏱ 40–50 min · Review & Extension
总结与拓展

Class Review (5 min)

  1. Can Fly Girl do tricks? → Yes, she can.
  2. Who eats grosser stuff? → Fly Girl.
  3. What is "fly talk" for "let's be friends"? → 引导学生说出苍蝇语（book 中画面音）并复述 "That is fly talk for 'Let's be friends.'"
  4. 用 3 个词总结本书主题 → friends / meet / nice

Extension (5 min)

🌟 拓展活动选择
  • Draw & Label: 画出你的"新朋友（宠物）"，标注名字和能力。
  • Role Play: 四人一组表演"公园相遇"场景（Buzz, Liz, Fly Guy, Fly Girl）。
