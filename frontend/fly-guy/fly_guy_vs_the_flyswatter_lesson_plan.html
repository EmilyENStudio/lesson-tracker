Overview

故事概览

Story Summary

A boy named Buzz has a pet fly named Fly Guy, who can say the boy's name. One morning Fly Guy is eating breakfast (chocolate!) in Buzz's backpack. At school, the teacher announces that the class is going on a field trip to tour a factory. Buzz lets Fly Guy ride along in his pocket.

The class takes the bus to the flyswatter factory. The tour guide shows them the flyswatter museum, and each child may take a flyswatter home. Then Fred the Fly — a worker who teaches visitors about flies — explains that flies play in slime and eat garbage, so people need "the flyswatter of the future: the Super Swatter 6000!" To show it off, the guide releases a tiny fly in a jar. The Super Swatter starts swatting, and little Fly Guy darts out to rescue the tiny fly, dodging swats as he carries it to an open window. Fred yells "Stop! Stop! No more factory tours, ever!" Back at school, the class makes an art project to finish their very eventful field trip.

📖 文本特征

  • 3 chapters, ~30 pages
  • 高频命令句：Release the fly! / Stop! Stop!
  • 反复句式（叠句）："The Super Swatter kept swatting."
  • 核心主题词：factory, flyswatter, garbage, slime, rescue

🎯 教学目标

  • Reading: 理解三章结构（出发-参观-事件），把握叠句对故事的推动作用
  • Language: 掌握核心词汇、命令/祈使句、can/may 用法、过去时
  • Speaking: 能复述参观经过、复现命令句、分角色朗读
  • Writing: 完成指令写作与意见表达：应不应该用 Super Swatter 6000？

LT02

Phonics / Decoding 语音拼读

  • sw- blend: swatter, swatting, swatted（本书灵魂词族）
  • fl- blend: fly, flew, flies

LT03

Pronunciation 发音

  • 音节切分：fac-to-ry (3), mu-se-um (3), gar-bage (2)
  • 重音模式：FAC-tory, mu-SE-um, GAR-bage
  • 双音节：nas-ty, slime, flyswatter
  • 连读：tour_the factory, bring_out the fly
LT04

Sentence Patterns 句型

  • 命令/祈使句：Release the fly! / Stop! Stop!
  • We are going on a field trip to tour a factory.
  • You can ride in my pocket. / You may each have one.
  • 叠句反复："The Super Swatter kept swatting."
LT05

Grammar 语法功能

  • 命令/祈使: Bring out the fly! Stay down!
  • can/may 能力与许可: ride in my pocket
  • Simple past: led, arrived, said, grabbed
  • kept + -ing: kept swatting（动作持续）
  • 一般现在描述: Flies play in slime. Flies eat garbage.
LT06

Morphology 词形变化

  • 双写辅音加 -ing/-ed: swat→swatting
  • -ing: eat→eating, ride→riding, fly→flying
  • 不规则过去式: lead→led, take→took, fly→flew, say→said
  • 复数 y→ies: fly→flies
  • 复合词: fly + swatter = flyswatter
LT07

Cohesion / Discourse 语篇衔接

  • 叠句反复: "The Super Swatter kept swatting." ×4
  • then: 时间顺序推进
  • 引语归属: said the guide / shouted Fred
  • 代词指代: "she"=tour guide, "he"=Fred/Fly Guy
  • 结尾衔接: field trip → 艺术课收尾
LT08

Functional Communication 语用功能

  • Give commands: 发指令（模拟 tour guide）
  • Describe: 描述工厂里看到的东西
  • Retell: 按顺序复述参观经过
  • Express opinion: flyswatter — 需要还是不需要？
  • Role-play: Fred the Fly vs. Fly Guy

⏱ 33–43 min · LT02 Phonics + LT03 Pronunciation
语音拼读与发音训练

Phonics Focus (5 min)

从本书中提取高频语音模式，引导学生发现规律：

辅音连缀 Consonant Blends — 本书灵魂词族 sw-

sw- swatter, swatting, swatted, swat

sw- 以 [s][w] 连读开头，嘴唇由扁变圆，像发"丝窝"。这个词族是全书冲突的来源。

更多辅音连缀

fl- fly, flew, flew past st- stay, started, stop sh- shouted, showed

-ing 后缀（动作进行/持续）

-ing /ɪŋ/ — swatting, eating, riding, flying

引导发现：swat → swatting（双写 t），eat → eating（直接加），ride → riding（去 e）。

⏱ 43–50 min · LT04 Sentence Patterns + Wrap-up
核心句型初探 + 小结

引入本课核心句式，为第二课时深度语法做铺垫：

[Verb] + (the) [noun]! — 命令/祈使句
"Release the fly!" / "Bring out the fly!" / "Stay down!" / "Stop! Stop!"

📌 命令句以动词原形开头，结尾常用感叹号，本书中多次出现。

We are going on a [noun] to [verb].
"We are going on a field trip to tour a factory."

📌 用 be going to 表达计划中的活动。

You can / may [verb].
"You can ride in my pocket." / "You may each have one."

📌 can 表能力，may 表许可，都可翻译为"你可以"。

The [noun] kept [verb]-ing.
"The Super Swatter kept swatting." ×4

📌 kept + -ing 表示持续做某事，是全书的叠句（refrain）。

📝 第一课时小结 回顾 Big Question: "Can Fly Guy protect his fly friends from the Super Swatter 6000?"
让学生用一句话表态："I think Fly Guy will ___ because ___."

⏱ 5–18 min · LT05 Grammar
语法功能深度分析

① 命令/祈使句 Imperatives (5 min)

本书命令句密集出现，是核心语法点：

原文句结构说话者→对象
"Stay down in my pocket!"动词原形 + 地点Buzz → Fly Guy
"Bring out the fly!"动词原形 + 宾语Fred → guide
"Release the fly!"动词原形 + 宾语Fred → guide
"Stop! Stop!"单个动词 + 重复Fred → everyone
"Let's see what the Super Swatter can do!"Let's + 动词原形Fred → class

练习：教师下达指令，学生表演动作——"Stand up! / Clap your hands! / Stop!"

② can / may — 能力与许可 (2 min)

原文句功能结构
"You can ride in my pocket."能力/允许（can）主语 + can + 动词原形
"You may each have one."许可（may）主语 + may + 动词原形
"What the Super Swatter can do"能力（can）主语 + can + 动词原形

练习：用 may 礼貌表达许可，用 can 表达能力。

③ Simple Past + kept + -ing (3 min)

原文语法点
"The class rode the bus." / "A guide led the class inside."不规则过去式：ride→rode, lead→led
"They arrived at the factory." / "Fly Guy smiled."规则过去式：-ed 结尾
"Fly Guy took the fly to an open window."不规则过去式：take→took
"The Super Swatter kept swatting."kept + -ing = 持续做某事
⚠️ 重点提示 kept swatting 是全书的叠句，讲的是动作"一再持续"。可以让学生用 "kept ___ing" 复述其他持续动作：kept flying, kept talking。

LT07 · Cohesion 语篇衔接 (4 min)
