Overview

故事概览

Story Summary

A young boy named Buzz has a pet fly named Fly Guy. One day Buzz goes to visit his grandma, and Fly Guy goes along. When Grandma bends down to hug Buzz, she accidentally swallows Fly Guy with a loud "Glurk!" Down he goes into a deep, dark hole and lands in a wet place at the bottom.

Fly Guy looks around and decides he wants to leave, but just as he starts back up the hole, Grandma swallows a spider to catch the fly, then a bird to catch the spider, then a cat to catch the bird, then a dog to catch the cat, a goat to catch the dog, and a cow to catch the goat! Just as Grandma is about to swallow a horse to catch the cow, Fly Guy cries out, "BUZZ!" Buzz yells, "I'm up here!" — and out comes Fly Guy, followed by the spider, the bird, the cat, the dog, the goat, and the cow. And everyone lives happily ever after, of course!

📖 文本特征

  • 3 chapters, 32 pages · classic nursery-rhyme parody
  • 连锁累积结构 (chaining): "swallowed a ___ to catch the ___"
  • 顺序连接词：First / Just then / Then ▸ 推动故事
  • 拟声词 onomatopoeia：Glurk! BUZZ!

🎯 教学目标

  • Reading: 理解连锁累积式故事结构，辨识事件发生的先后顺序
  • Language: 掌握核心词汇、to catch 表目的、过去时态
  • Speaking: 能按顺序复述连锁故事（first, then, next）
  • Writing: 仿写自己的老奶奶吞飞物连锁小故事

LT 框架总览 — There Was an Old Lady Who Swallowed Fly Guy

以下 8 个模块构成本书的完整语言目标体系，两个课时分工推进。

LT01

Vocabulary 词汇

12 个核心词，含 IPA、词性、搭配、例句、词族。

  • swallow, catch, spider, hole, deep
  • wet, leave, climb, goat, cow, horse
  • worried
LT02

Phonics / Decoding 语音拼读

  • sw- blend: swallow, swallowed
  • -ed past suffix: swallowed, started, wanted, looked
  • Short vowel CVC: cat, dog, wet, big
  • -ow vowel team /aʊ/: cow, down, how, now
  • -oa long vowel: goat
LT03

Pronunciation 发音

  • 音节切分：swal-low (2), spi-der (2), e-ver-y-one (3/4)
  • 重音模式：SWAL-low, SPY-der, a-BOUT
  • 连读：swallowed_a spider · caught_the fly
  • 尾音 -(e)d 三态：/d/ looked / /t/ swallowed ▸ 视词形而定
LT04

Sentence Patterns 句型

  • She swallowed a [noun] to catch the [noun].
  • He wanted to [verb].
  • Just then, ___ . / Then ___ .
  • Out came the [animal]. (倒装)
  • 第一课 First... Then... In the end...
LT05

Grammar 语法功能

  • Simple past: swallowed, started, wanted, looked, cried, yelled, came
  • to + 动词表目的 (purpose): "swallowed a spider to catch the fly"
  • be about to (即将): "Grandma was about to swallow a horse."
  • 顺序/序数: First, Just then, Then, In the end
  • Prepositions: down, up, out of, at the bottom of
LT06

Morphology 词形变化

  • -ed 规则过去式: swallow→swallowed, start→started, want→wanted, look→looked
  • 不规则过去式: catch→caught, come→came, cry→cried, say→said
  • 复数 -s: spider→spiders, animal→animals
  • 合成词: every + one = everyone
LT07

Cohesion / Discourse 语篇衔接

  • 连锁目的从句 (chaining): to catch the spider ▸ to catch the bird ▸ to catch the cat…
  • 顺序连接词: One day, Just then, Then, In the end
  • 代词指代: "she" = Grandma; "he" = Fly Guy
  • 倒装衔接: "Out came Fly Guy."
  • 结尾公式: "lived happily ever after."
LT08

Functional Communication 语用功能

  • Sequence/Retell: 按顺序复述连锁故事
  • Describe: 描述各个动物及大小排序
  • Explain: 为什么奶奶一个个吞动物
  • Create: 仿写自己的连锁小故事
  • Express opinion: 你觉得哪个动物最滑稽，为什么?

⏱ 10–20 min · Read Aloud
通读全文 · 理解故事

Chapter 1 精读 (5 min)

页面原文教学重点
p.2–4 "A young boy named Buzz had a pet fly. No one knows why he had a pet fly. Buzz named him Fly Guy." 复习前册人物；理解 why 句型
p.5–7 "One day Buzz went to visit his grandma. Fly Guy went, too. Grandma ran to hug him." 理解 went to visit；too 的用法；one day 开启故事
p.8–10 "'Hi, Grandma!' said Buzz. 'I want you to meet my pet...' and she swallowed Fly Guy." 理解吞入的意外时刻；"Glurk!" 拟声词
p.11 "Buzz didn't know why she swallowed Fly Guy." 引出悬念；复现书名的经典反问结构

Chapter 2 与 Chapter 3 精读 (5 min)

页面原文教学重点
p.12–14 "Fly Guy went down a deep dark hole. At the bottom of the hole, he came to a wet place. He looked around for a while. Then he wanted to leave." needs: down/bottom/wet；方位介词
p.15–16 "He started up the hole. Just then, Grandma swallowed a spider to catch the fly." 核心句型 "swallowed a spider to catch the fly"；连锁开始
p.17–24 "She swallowed a bird to catch the spider. She swallowed a cat to catch the bird. She swallowed a dog... a goat... a cow." 连锁累积结构；动物由小到大排序
p.25–27 "Grandma was about to swallow a horse to catch the cow. Fly Guy cried, BUZZ!" be about to；高潮悬念；BUZZ! 呼救
p.28–30 "'I'm up here!' yelled Buzz. Out came Fly Guy. Out came the spider, the bird, the cat, the dog, the goat, and the cow. And everyone lived happily ever after, of course!" 倒装 Out came；结尾公式 happily ever after
💡 跟读技巧 连锁句子越长越好读——让学生接力：每读到一个新动物就加入前面所有动物，体会累积的节奏感与画面感。

③ be about to — 即将发生 (3 min)

主语 + was/were about to + 动词原形
"Grandma was about to swallow a horse to catch the cow."

表示"正要做、马上要做"，用法与 be going to 相近但更强调"即将"。这是制造悬紧张的关键结构。

LT07 · Cohesion 语篇衔接 (4 min)

分析本书中的衔接手段，理解作者如何连接句子、推动连锁故事：

DeviceFunctionExample from Text
chain (to catch the ___) 连锁目的从句 — 全书最核心 "...a spider to catch the fly... a bird to catch the spider..."
just then / then 顺序递进 sequence "Just then, Grandma swallowed a spider." / "Then he wanted to leave."
代词 she / he 指代 reference — 需要看上下文 "She swallowed..." → She = Grandma
"He wanted to leave." → He = Fly Guy
Out came... 倒装 + 收束 inversion + closure "Out came the spider, the bird, the cat..."
happily ever after 结尾公式 story-end formula "And everyone lived happily ever after, of course!"
💡 讨论：连锁的魔法 让学生找出整条链：spider ▸ bird ▸ cat ▸ dog ▸ goat ▸ cow。讨论：每次新动物为什么出现？ → 都是为了 to catch 上一个动物，一环套一环，画面越滚越大。
⏱ 26–40 min · LT08 Functional Communication
口语输出与写作任务

Activity 1: Chain Retell (5 min)

学生两人一组，用以下框架按顺序复述连锁故事：

Chain Retelling Frame:

First, Buzz took Fly Guy to visit Grandma and she swallowed Fly Guy.
Then, Grandma swallowed a spider to catch the fly.
Next, she swallowed a bird to catch the spider...
After that, she was about to swallow a horse to catch the cow.
In the end, Fly Guy cried "BUZZ!", Buzz yelled, and out they all came!

Activity 2: Size the Animals (5 min)

回到连锁关键词，进行动物排序小游戏：

Discussion Prompts:

  • "Which animal is smallest? Which is biggest?" → Spider < bird < cat < dog < goat < cow.
  • "Why did Grandma keep swallowing bigger and bigger animals?" → To catch the one before; it got silly!
  • "Which animal do you think is the funniest? Why?"

Language scaffold:

"I think the ___ is the funniest because ___."
"The spider is smaller than the bird."
"She swallowed the cow to catch the goat."

Activity 3: Writing — My Own Old Lady (4 min 指导 + 课后完成)

Writing Prompt:

Write your own silly chain story starting with "There was an old lady who swallowed a ___."

Writing Frame (scaffold):

There was an old lady who swallowed a ___ to catch the ___.
Just then, she swallowed a ___ to catch the ___.
Then she swallowed a ___ to catch the ___.
In the end, out came the ___, the ___, and the ___!
