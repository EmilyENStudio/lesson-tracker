Overview

故事概览

Story Summary

Fly Guy has learned three amazing new tricks — The Backstroke, The Dizzy Doozie, and The Big Booger. Buzz puts on a show for his friends called The Amazing Fly Guy Circus and calls out each trick with the signal word "Now!"

But when supper comes, Fly Guy shows off the same tricks in Mom's milk, around Dad's head, and on the dinner table — making a terrible mess. So Buzz gives Fly Guy a simple rule: "Do your tricks only when you hear the word NOW." That night, a big kid tries to bully Buzz and teases him, "Bug got your tongue?" Fly Guy hears the magic word "NOW!" and lets loose the full circus — scaring the kid away. Buzz finishes with a brand-new trick: "High Fivezzz!"

📖 文本特征

  • 3 chapters, ~30 pages
  • 重复指令："Now," said Buzz ×3 — 同一句式反复驱动情节
  • 三个命名的把戏 (tricks) 反复出现：Backstroke / Dizzy Doozie / Big Booger
  • 关键信号词：NOW — 把"混乱"和"受控"两半故事连接起来
  • 口语习语："Bug got your tongue?" / "Get out of my face!"
  • 文字游戏 -zz 结尾：BUZZ! / YEZZZ! / High Fivezzz!

🎯 教学目标

  • Reading: 理清故事顺序（First / Then / And then）与"NOW 规则"的因果逻辑
  • Language: 掌握祈使句、命令、"didn't + 动词原形"、-zz 拼读规律
  • Speaking: 能用指令词发令、复述三个把戏、回应挑衅
  • Writing: 完成 opinion / recount 写作：Should Fly Guy follow Buzz's rule?
Language Target Framework

LT 框架总览 — Fly Guy's Amazing Tricks

以下 8 个模块构成本书的完整语言目标体系，两个课时分工推进。

LT01

Vocabulary 词汇

12 个核心词，含 IPA、词性、搭配、例句、词族。

  • trick, circus, backstroke, dizzy, booger
  • supper, mess, idea, answer, yell, chase, tongue
LT02

Phonics / Decoding 语音拼读

  • fl- blend: fly, flew, flying
  • -zz buzz sound: BUZZ, YEZZZ, Fivezzz
  • st- blend: stop, started
  • ow diphthong: now, how
  • -ing: talking, flying
  • Short a / e / i: back, mess, dizzy
LT03

Pronunciation 发音

  • 音节切分：back-stroke (2), diz-zy (2), cir-cus (2)
  • 重音模式：BACK-stroke, CIR-cus, YEZZZ
  • 信号词强调：重读 "NOW!"，声调上扬
  • 单词尾 -zz 的连读与延长音
LT04

Sentence Patterns 句型

  • "Now," said Buzz, "The Backstroke!" (say 倒装)
  • Do your tricks only when [条件].
  • ___ didn't answer. (过去否定)
  • "Time for supper," said Mom.
  • 反复：Now, now, and now!
LT05

Grammar 语法功能

  • Imperative 祈使: "Stop!" "Answer me!" "Let's clean up."
  • 过去否定: didn't + 动词原形 (Buzz didn't answer ×3)
  • say-句倒装: "Now," said Buzz
  • 条件指令: only when you hear
  • 代词: him — 有的指 Buzz，有的指 Fly Guy
LT06

Morphology 词形变化

  • -s 复数: trick→tricks, friend→friends
  • f→ves 特殊复: fly→flies
  • -ed 过去式: learn→learned, yell→yelled, chased
  • 不规则过去式: hear→heard, say→said, catch→caught
  • -zz 拟声: BUZZ, YEZZZ, Fivezzz
LT07

Cohesion / Discourse 语篇衔接

  • 顺序词: First / Then / And then (三把戏顺序)
  • 信号重复: "Now," said Buzz 串起整个演出
  • 代词指代: him = Buzz / Fly Guy 需推断
  • 句式节奏: "Buzz didn't answer." ×3 制造张力
  • 因果: NOW 规则 → 受控表演 → 吓跑大孩子
LT08

Functional Communication 语用功能

  • Give commands: "Stop!" "Do your trick!"
  • Retell: What happened first / next / at the end?
  • Follow a signal word: listen for NOW!
  • Respond to teasing: What could Buzz say to the big kid?
  • Express opinion: Should Fly Guy do tricks only on "NOW"?

① Imperative 祈使句 — 发出命令 (5 min)

LT07 · Cohesion 语篇衔接 (4 min)

分析本书中的衔接手段，理解作者如何组织故事、制造张力：

DeviceFunctionExample from Text
顺序词 sequence — 按顺序推进演出 "First The Backstroke. Then The Dizzy Doozie. And then The Big Booger."
信号重复 repetition — 用同一句连贯演出 "Now," said Buzz ×3
代词 him reference — 需看上下文推断 "Fly Guy didn't hear him." → him = Buzz

⏱ 26–40 min · LT08 Functional Communication
口语输出与写作任务

Activity 1: Retell the Three Tricks (5 min)

学生两人一组，用以下框架复述演出顺序：

Sequence Frame:

First, Buzz said, "The Backstroke!"
Then, Buzz said, "The Dizzy Doozie!"
And then, Buzz said, "The Big Booger!"
At supper, Fly Guy did the tricks again — but this time nobody said "NOW!"
So Buzz said, "Do your tricks only when you hear the word NOW."
In the end, Fly Guy heard "NOW" and chased the big kid away. High Fivezzz!

Activity 2: Give a Command — Follow the Signal (5 min)

让学生做"小小导演"：一人用祈使句发令，另一人表演：

Role-Play Prompts:

  • "Stop, Fly Guy!" → Stop moving.
  • "Clean up this mess!" → Pretend to tidy up.
  • "Answer me!" → Say a word.
  • "Now! The Backstroke!" → Do a swimming motion.

Practice question:

"Why does Fly Guy only do his tricks when he hears the word NOW?"

Activity 3: Responding to Teasing + Opinion (4 min 指导 + 课后完成)

Talk about it:

"Bug got your tongue?" — What does the big kid mean? How does Buzz stay calm? What could Buzz say back?

Opinion prompt (for homework):

✅ Answer Key

Sample answer:

I think the NOW rule is a good idea because Fly Guy made a big mess at supper. First, he did his tricks in Mom's milk and around Dad's head. Also, nobody knew when the tricks would happen, so it was chaos. In conclusion, doing tricks only on the word NOW is the smart and tidy way to win the circus show.
