故事概览Overview

故事概览Story Overview

Story Summary

A boy has a pet fly named Fly Guy. Fly Guy can say the boy's name — "BUZZ!" One night, Buzz tells Fly Guy, "I made a book. We are the superheroes." He settles in to read the book aloud to his tiny friend.

Inside Buzz's comic, "Buzz Boy and Fly Guy," Buzz Boy is a superhero who is the same size as Fly Guy. Bad pirates put Buzz Boy and Fly Guy in jail on a pirate ship. Then a dragon shows up — "GOOD THING WE ARE SUPERHEROES!" With his superstrength, Buzz Boy turns the dragon around, and the dragon carries their house back home. "The end," says Buzz. Fly Guy answers happily — "BUZZ!" "Superheroes," says Buzz. "Want to read it again?"

📖 文本特征 / Text Features

  • Story within a story — 双层叙事结构（frame narrative）
  • 漫画式文体：大写字母 = 喊叫（comic typography）
  • 超英主题词：superhero, superstrength, cape, adventure
  • 核心句型：We are ___ . / I'll read it to you. / With his superstrength…

🎯 教学目标 / Teaching Goals

  • Reading: 区分"现实层"与"故事层"，梳理冒险顺序
  • Language: be+身份、不规则过去时、will 将来、被动结构
  • Speaking: 复述漫画冒险、表达对超级英雄的看法
  • Writing: 自制一页迷你漫画（superhero comic）

Vocabulary 词汇

LT02

Phonics / Decoding 语音拼读

  • dr- blend: dragon, drove
  • sh- digraph: ship, shine, brush
  • -ay (long a): say, day, today
  • -ee (long e): read, see, need
  • -ing suffix: reading, flying
  • super- prefix: superhero, superstrength
LT03

Pronunciation 发音

  • 音节切分：su-per-he-ro (4), a-gain (2), dra-gon (2)
  • 重音模式：SUperhero, SUperstrength, aGAIN
  • 句重音：大写 = 重读喊叫（GOOD THING WE ARE SUPERHEROES!）
  • 连读：read_it to you, turned_the dragon
LT04

Sentence Patterns 句型

  • We are [noun]s. (be + 身份)
  • I made a [noun]. / I'll read it to you.
  • [Subject] were put in jail on the [place]. (被动)
  • With his superstrength, [Subject] turned the [noun] around.
  • Want to read it again? (省略问句)
  • 大写喊叫：GOOD THING WE ARE SUPERHEROES!
LT05

Grammar 语法功能

  • be + 身份: "We are the superheroes."
  • Irregular past: made, said, took, read, put, was
  • Future (will): "I'll read it to you."
  • Passive: "were put in jail on the pirate ship."
  • With + NP: "With his superstrength…"
LT06

Morphology 词形变化

  • super- 前缀: hero→superhero, strength→superstrength
  • 复合词: super + hero
  • -s 复数: heroes, pirates, books (hero→heroes)
  • Irregular past: make→made, take→took, read→read, say→said, put→put
  • -ing: fly→flying, read→reading
LT07

Cohesion / Discourse 语篇衔接

  • 双层叙事: 现实层 ↔ 漫画层转换
  • 时间状语: "One night Buzz said…"
  • 顺序: same size → jail → dragon → home
  • 代词指代: we (Buzz+Fly Guy), I (Buzz), it (book), their
  • 首尾呼应: "Want to read it again?"
LT08

Functional Communication 语用功能

  • Describe: Describe Buzz Boy (the tiny superhero)
  • Retell: Retell Buzz's comic adventure in order
  • Predict: What will happen next in the comic?
  • Create: Make your own one-page superhero comic
  • Express opinion: Would you want to be a superhero?

页面原文教学重点

内层漫画 · The Comic Adventure (5 min)The Comic Adventure (5 min)

页面原文教学重点
漫画开场 "BUZZ BOY AND FLY GUY. HE WAS THE SAME SIZE AS FLY GUY!" 引出同尺寸 joke (superhero = 苍蝇大小)；"the same size as" 比较结构
遇险 "BUZZ BOY AND FLY GUY WERE PUT IN JAIL ON THE PIRATE SHIP." 被动结构 "were put in jail"；pirate / jail / ship 词汇
龙来袭 "GOOD THING WE ARE SUPERHEROES!" 大写 = 喊叫 (comic typography)；dragon 词汇
反击 "WITH HIS SUPERSTRENGTH, BUZZ BOY TURNED THE DRAGON AROUND." With + superstrength (工具)；短语动词 turned...around
结局 "THE DRAGON TOOK THEIR HOUSE BACK HOME." 不规则过去 took；their house / back home
返回现实 "'The end,' said Buzz. Fly Guy said — BUZZ!" · "'Superheroes,' said Buzz. 'Want to read it again?'" 首尾呼应；Fly Guy 的回应离开现实层，课堂追问"故事真的结束了吗?"

用这些句型帮助你：Use these sentence frames to help:

This is ___. He/She is the same size as ___.
Once, ___ happened. Good thing we are superheroes!
With his/her superstrength, ___ turned ___ around.
In the end, ___.

Model Answer: "This is Mia Boy. He is the same size as a ladybug. Once, a giant robot came to town. Good thing we are superheroes! With his superstrength, Mia Boy turned the robot around. In the end, the robot went back to space."
