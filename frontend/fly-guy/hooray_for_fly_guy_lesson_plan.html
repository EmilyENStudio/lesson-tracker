故事概览Story Overview

Story Summary

Buzz has a pet fly named Fly Guy, who can say the boy's name — "BUZZ!" One day Fly Guy goes with Buzz to play football. Coach says, "We need one more player for the big game." Buzz proudly announces, "Fly Guy can play!" Coach laughs, "Flies can't play football." But Fly Guy shows what he can do — he kicks the ball, goes out for a pass, and even tries to tackle Buzz. Coach finally admits, "I was right. Flies can't play football. But he can come to the game."

The day before the big game, Buzz makes Fly Guy a tiny helmet. The team plays football, does jumping jacks, plans a secret play, and makes up a touchdown dance. At the big game, Fly Guy sits on the bench and cheers. It looks bad — with one second left, the other team is about to score again, and the team's new player gets hurt. Coach says, "Okay, Fly Guy. You can play now. The game is lost anyway." Buzz calls out, "It's time for our secret play!" Fly Guy flies fast and straight — right up the quarterback's nose! The quarterback drops the ball, Buzz picks it up and runs for a touchdown, and Fly Guy and Buzz do their touchdown dance. The whole team cheers: "We won! Hooray for Fly Guy!"

📖 文本特征Text Features

  • 2 chapters, ~30 pages
  • 重复/平行句式Repetition: "Fly Guy can play." / "Flies can't play football."
  • 并列动作序列Parallel action: They played / did / planned / made up...
  • 核心主题Theme: teamwork, never judging by size, believing in a friend

  • Reading: 理解故事结构与顺序understand story sequence and key details
  • Language: 掌握核心词汇、can/can't 能力表达、过去时态core vocabulary, can/can't, past tense
  • Speaking: 能复述关键情节、表达观点retell key events and share opinions
  • Writing: 完成观点写作：Can a fly play football?opinion writing: Can a fly play football?

LT04

Sentence Patterns 句型

  • ___ can / can't [verb]. —"Fly Guy can play."
  • "Flies can't play football." (否定分类)
  • The team [verb] and [verb]. —"They planned / made up..."
  • "It's time for [noun]." —"It's time for our secret play."
  • 习惯用语：Hooray for ___!
LT05

Grammar 语法功能

  • Ability: can / can't — "Fly Guy can play."
  • Simple past (regular): kicked, scored, cheered, planned, worried
  • Simple past (irregular): went, said, made, did, ran, flew, won, got
  • Future intent: "They were about to score again."
  • Imperatives/cheers: "Hooray for Fly Guy!"
LT06

Morphology 词形变化

  • -ed 规则过去式: kick→kicked, play→played, cheer→cheered
  • 双写辅音 + ed: plan→planned, snap→snapped
  • -y → -ied: worry→worried
  • 不规则过去式: go→went, fly→flew, run→ran, win→won
  • 复合词: foot+ball, touch+down, quarter+back
LT07

Cohesion / Discourse 语篇衔接

  • but (转折): "Flies can't play football. But he can come to the game."
  • and (并列/顺承): "picked it up and ran"
  • 时间词: Finally, The game started, Then
  • 代词指代: "he" 指 Fly Guy / Buzz / quarterback?
  • 感叹呼应: "Hooray for Fly Guy!" 首尾呼应
LT08

Functional Communication 语用功能

  • Describe: Describe Fly Guy the football player.
  • Retell: What happened first / next / at the end?
  • Predict: What is the secret play?
  • Express opinion: Can a fly really play football?
  • Cheer/celebrate: Make up your own cheer with "Hooray for ___!"
