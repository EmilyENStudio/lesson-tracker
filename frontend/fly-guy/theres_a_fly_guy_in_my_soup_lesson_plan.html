Overview

故事概览

Story Summary

A boy has a pet fly named Fly Guy, and Fly Guy can say the boy's name — BUZZ! One day Buzz goes on a long trip with Mom and Dad. They stop at a hotel for dinner. There is a nice restaurant downstairs, but Fly Guy can't go inside, so Buzz says he can eat outside.

Fly Guy flies out. He finds a trash can, a puddle, a sticky spot, and the biggest, slimiest garbage can ever — but not one thing he wants to eat. Then he smells something wonderful and follows the smell all the way to a small, round bathtub full of warm brown soup. He needs to wash before dinner, so in he hops to wash his face and hands, his armpits, and between his toes. Perfect!

Then the bathtub is picked up and set on a table in front of a lady — who screams, "Waiter! There's a fly in my soup!" Soup and Fly Guy go flying... into another lady's soup — and then a gentleman's hair — until everyone is jumping up and everyone needs a bath. "Last one in the pool is a rotten egg!" yells Buzz, and all the soup-caked travelers dive in.

📖 文本特征

  • 3 章，约 30 页
  • 反复句式：He found... / ...went flying into...
  • 关键修辞：连还/累积重复 (cumulative repetition)、拟声 (OOPZ! MEZZY!)
  • 核心主题词：hotel, soup, bath, wash, There's...

🎯 教学目标

  • Reading: 理解故事因果链与连还重复结构，提取关键细节
  • Language: 掌握 There is/There's、wash 表达、过去时态
  • Speaking: 能用 There's... 描述情境、复述连锁事件
  • Writing: 完成情境写作："There's a fly in my soup!"

LT05

Grammar 语法功能

  • 存在句：There is / There's
  • 能力/许可：can / can't
  • Simple past：went, drove, found, smelled, washed
  • need to + verb
  • 被动：was picked up / was set down
LT06

Morphology 词形变化

  • -ed：followed, smelled, washed, screamed, jumped
  • 不规则过去：go→went, drive→drove, find→found, fly→flew
  • -ing：flying, washing
  • 复合词：down+stairs, bath+tub, garbage+can, gentle+man
  • 复数：hands, toes
LT07

Cohesion / Discourse 语篇衔接

  • but (转折)：but Fly Guy can't go there
  • then / at last：时间顺序
  • 累积重复：went flying into... 连锁句逐次叠加细节
  • 代词指代：he / they / it
LT08

Functional Communication 语用功能

  • Describe：描述酒店、汤、混乱场面
  • Retell：复述连锁事件
  • Predict：猜测女士会怎么办

  • Opinion：在汤里洗澡是不是好主意？

Phonics Focus (5 min)

从本书中提取高频语音模式，引导学生发现规律：

sh 字母组合（清音 /ʃ/）

sh wash, washing, fish sh- shoo

舌尖靠近上齿龈，气流摩擦送出，声带不振动。

oo 长元音 /uː/

oo room, cool, food

发现规律：oo 在大多数单词里读长音 /uː/，圆唇长元音。

-ing 后缀

-ing /ɪŋ/ — flying, going, washing, something

动词 + ing = 正在做 / 动作进行。

ow / ou 双元音 /aʊ/

down downstairs, down ou found, around

由 /a/ 滑向 /ʊ/，下巴先张大后收拢。

找连锁句！

从书中抄写一句以 "went flying" 结尾的句子，并写出它一共出现了几次。

Sentence:

It repeats times.

参考答案 Answer Key

1. Fly Guy · 2. Fly Guy · 3. the bathtub (the bowl of soup Fly Guy is in) · 4. the first lady · 5. all the people in the restaurant

Chain example: "Her soup and Fly Guy went flying into another lady's soup." The went flying pattern repeats four times (into another lady's soup → into a gentleman's head → and it keeps building), each time more soup and more people join the chaos.

Teacher note: accept any correctly copied chain sentence; the point is that students notice the cumulative repetition.

Part F · Creative Writing — A Funny Scene
LT08 · 写一个"有东西不该出现在某处"的搞笑场景，至少 5 句，必须用到本课的句型。

可用的句型：

"There's a ___ in my ___!"
"First, I was ___. Then I saw ___."
"OOPZ! MEZZY! Oh no!"
"In the end, everyone ___."

参考答案（示例短文）Answer Key

Sample (native-level model):

"First, I was eating breakfast with my family. Then I saw something wiggling in my cereal bowl. 'There's a frog in my milk!' I yelled. My little sister dropped her spoon, and the frog jumped right onto Dad's toast. OOPZ! Everyone started shouting, but the frog just sat there looking as happy as can be. In the end, we took him outside and let him hop away — but now Dad always checks his toast!"

Checklist: target phrase included? · 5+ sentences? · a silly surprise + a clear ending?
