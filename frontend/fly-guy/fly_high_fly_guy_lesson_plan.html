Overview

故事概览

Story Summary

Buzz announces, "It's time to take a road trip!" Fly Guy wants to come along, but Mom and Dad say, "He's too little. He might get lost. Fly Guy stays home." Yet when the family stops for a picnic and Mom opens the trunk — surprise! Out flies Fly Guy. The family drives to the beach, the art museum, and the fun park, and at every stop the same question comes up: "Is Fly Guy lost?" "No," says Buzz, "here he is!"

On the drive home they go and go, but they can't find their way — "We're lost!" Buzz and Fly Guy get an idea. "Fly high, Fly Guy!" Fly Guy soars into the sky and uses his super fly eyes to spy their house, then leads the whole family home. "Thank you, Fly Guy. You saved the day!" — and in a playful twist... "Oh, no! Is Fly Guy lost?"

📖 文本特征

  • 3 chapters, ~32 pages (Cartwheel Book, 2008)
  • 重复句式：Is Fly Guy lost? / No, here he is!
  • 关键修辞手法：反复 (repetition)、叠词 (drove and drove)
  • 核心主题词：road trip, lost, rescue, tiny friend, big help

🎯 教学目标

  • Reading: 循路线图读懂旅途故事，理解"问-答"重复结构与问题-解决结构
  • Language: 掌握 might / too + adj / Let's / 一般过去式（规则与不规则）
  • Speaking: 能复述旅途、顺势预测、表达观点（Should Fly Guy have come?）
  • Writing: 完成 problem & solution 写作：小帮手如何化解危机
Language Target Framework

LT 框架总览 — Fly High, Fly Guy!

以下 8 个模块构成本书的完整语言目标体系，两个课时分工推进。

LT01

Vocabulary 词汇

12 个核心词，含 IPA、词性、搭配、例句、词族。

  • road trip, lost, little, trunk, picnic
  • beach, museum, fun park, idea, spy
  • super, rescue
LT02

Phonics / Decoding 语音拼读

  • igh (long i): fly, high, flies
  • dr- blend: drove, drive
  • tr- blend: trip, trunk
  • sp- blend: spy
  • -ay vowel team: way, day
  • th function words: the, they, then
LT03

Pronunciation 发音

  • 音节切分：pi-cnic (2), mu-SE-um (3), i-DE-a (3)
  • 重音模式：PI-cnic, mu-SE-um, i-DE-a
  • 复合词重读：ROAD trip, FUN park
  • 连读：drove_to the beach, time_to go
LT04

Sentence Patterns 句型

  • It's time to [verb]. — "time to take a road trip"
  • Let's [verb]! — "Let's hit the road!"
  • He might [verb]. — "He might get lost."
  • He's too [adj]. — "He's too little."
  • Is ___ lost? — "Is Fly Guy lost?"
  • 反复：drove and drove and drove
LT05

Grammar 语法功能

  • Possibility: might — "He might get lost."
  • too + adjective: "He's too little."
  • Suggestion: Let's — "Let's hit the road!"
  • Imperative: "Just don't lose him."
  • Simple past: said, shut, drove, stopped, opened, flew, led
  • Yes/No questions: "Is Fly Guy lost?"
LT06

Morphology 词形变化

  • Regular -ed: stop→stopped, want→wanted, open→opened
  • Irregular past: say→said, shut→shut, drive→drove, fly→flew, lead→led, come→came
  • Word families: fly / flew / flown; drive / drove / driven
  • Compound phrases: road trip, fun park, super fly eyes
LT07

Cohesion / Discourse 语篇衔接

  • and (反复并列): "They drove and drove and drove."
  • then (顺承): "Then it was time to go." ×3
  • but (转折): "But they did not get home."
  • 问-答重复链: "Is Fly Guy lost?" / "No, here he is!"
  • 代词指代: he = Fly Guy
  • 问题-解决结构 (problem → solution)
LT08

Functional Communication 语用功能

  • Retell: 复述旅途：先到哪，再到哪，最后怎样
  • Problem & Solution: 问题是什么？Fly Guy 如何帮忙？
  • Express opinion: Should Fly Guy have come on the trip?
  • Predict: 结尾 "Is Fly Guy lost?" 他真的丢了吗？
  • Describe: Fly Guy 的超能力是什么？

Phonics Focus (5 min)

从本书中提取高频语音模式，引导学生发现规律：

igh — 长音 /aɪ/

辅音连缀 Consonant Blends

dr- drove, drive tr- trip, trunk sp- spy

连缀是两个音快速连读：dr-, tr-, sp-。找一找：drove / trip / spy。

-ay 与 th

-ay /eɪ/ — way, day, stay th /ð/ — the, they, then

-ay 发 /eɪ/（way, saved the day, stays home）；th 舌尖轻触上齿（they, then, the）。

语法功能深度分析

① might — 表"可能" (3 min)

原文句功能结构
"He might get lost."可能（不一定）主语 + might + 动词原形
"We might be late."可能（推测）主语 + might + be + …

练习：用 might 表达可能。"Fly Guy might ___." / "We might ___."

② too + adjective — 表"太……" (3 min)

He's too [adjective] to [verb].
"He's too little." → He's too little to come along.

too big / too slow / too small ✓ —— 而 very 也可强调，但 too 表示"过了头"。

③ Let's / Don't — 建议与命令 (2 min)

Let's [verb]!   |   (Just) Don't [verb].
"Let's hit the road!" / "Just don't lose him."

Let's = let us（一起做）；Don't + 动词 = 别做……。

Activity 1: Retell the Journey (5 min)

First, Buzz said, "It's time to take a road trip."

Next, they drove to the beach, the art museum, and the fun park.
At each stop, Mom and Dad said, "Is Fly Guy lost?" "No, here he is!"

Activity 2: Problem & Solution (5 min)

Discussion Prompts:

  • "What was the family's problem?" → They were lost.
  • "Who solved it? How?" → Fly Guy flew high and spied the house.
  • "What is Fly Guy's super power?" → his super fly eyes / flying high.
  • "Do you think 'too little' can still help? Why?"

Language scaffold:

"The problem was ___."
"Fly Guy helped by ___-ing."
"In my opinion, ___."

Activity 3: Writing — Problem & Solution (4 min 指导 + 课后完成)

Writing Frame (scaffold):

✅ Answer Key (Teacher)
  1. Mom and Dad
  2. Buzz
  3. he = Fly Guy
  4. "They drove and drove and drove."
  5. "But they did not get home." → Before "but": the family was happily driving home. After "but": they are lost — "We're lost!" The but turns a smooth ride into a problem, which leads to the climax.
Part F · Problem & Solution Writing
LT08 · The family was lost. How did Fly Guy save the day? Write 4–6 sentences.

Use these sentence starters if you need help:

First, ___.
The problem was ___.
Then Buzz said, "___!"
Fly Guy flew __-ing and spied ___.
In the end, ___.
I think Fly Guy is a hero because ___.

✅ Answer Key (Teacher · Sample Answer)

Model answer:

First, the family drove all the way home from the beach, the museum, and the fun park. The problem was that they got lost — they could not find their way home. Then Buzz had an idea, and he said, "Fly high, Fly Guy!" Fly Guy flew high into the sky, used his super fly eyes to spy their house, and led the way home. In the end, Mom and Dad said, "Thank you, Fly Guy. You saved the day!" I think Fly Guy is a hero because he is small but he helped the whole family.

Mark for: a clear problem (did not get home / were lost), a clear solution (flew high, spied, led home), and correctly used past tense.
