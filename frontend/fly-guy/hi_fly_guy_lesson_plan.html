Overview

故事概览

Story Summary

A fly goes flying looking for something tasty and slimy to eat. A boy named Buzz goes walking looking for something smart for The Amazing Pet Show. Buzz catches the fly in a jar. The fly is mad and stomps his foot, saying "BUZZ!" — which happens to be the boy's name! Buzz thinks the fly is the smartest pet in the world.

Buzz brings Fly Guy home. Dad says flies are pests, not pets, and grabs a fly swatter. But Fly Guy says "BUZZ!" and Buzz rescues him. Mom names the fly "Fly Guy." They go to The Amazing Pet Show. The judges laugh at first — "Flies can't be pets!" — but Fly Guy proves himself with fancy flying tricks, says "BUZZ!" to show he knows his boy's name, and dives perfectly back into his jar. The judges are won over. Fly Guy wins an award, and a beautiful friendship begins.

📖 文本特征

  • 2 chapters, ~30 pages
  • 重复句式：A ___ went ___. / He was looking for...
  • 关键修辞手法：反复 (repetition)、对比 (pest vs. pet)
  • 核心主题词：friendship, pet vs. pest, proving yourself

🎯 教学目标

  • Reading: 理解故事结构（起因-经过-高潮-结局），提取关键细节
  • Language: 掌握核心词汇、过去时态、can/can't 能力表达
  • Speaking: 能复述故事、表达对角色的看法
  • Writing: 完成 opinion 写作：Is a fly a pest or a pet?
Language Target Framework

LT 框架总览 — Hi! Fly Guy

以下 8 个模块构成本书的完整语言目标体系，两个课时分工推进。

LT01

Vocabulary 词汇

12 个核心词，含 IPA、词性、搭配、例句、词族。

  • fly, catch, jar, pet, pest, swatter
  • slimy, tasty, smart, amazed, fancy
  • rescue, friendship, judges, award
LT02

Phonics / Decoding 语音拼读

  • fl- blend: fly, flying, flew
  • st- blend: stomp, started
  • sm- blend: smart
  • sh-: shoo
  • th function words: the, this, that, they
  • Short a: a fly, a jar, a pet, amazing
LT03

Pronunciation 发音

  • 音节切分：fly-ing (2), a-maz-ing (3), sur-prised (2)
  • 重音模式：aMAZing, surPRISED, REScue
  • 句重音：content words 重读
  • 连读：caught_the fly, took_the fly
LT04

Sentence Patterns 句型

  • A [noun] went [verb]-ing.
  • He was looking for something to [verb].
  • ___ can / can't [verb].
  • Flies can't be pets. (否定分类)
  • 反复修辞：high, high, high / down, down, down
LT05

Grammar 语法功能

  • Ability: can / can't — "He can say my name."
  • Simple past: caught, said, flew, stomped, took
  • be 动词: is, are, was — "This fly is smart."
  • Superlative: the smartest pet
  • Pronouns: he (指 Fly Guy 还是 Buzz? 上下文推断)
LT06

Morphology 词形变化

  • -ing: fly→flying, walk→walking, look→looking
  • -ed: stomp→stomped, amaze→amazed, surprise→surprised
  • -est: smart→smartest
  • Irregular past: catch→caught, say→said, fly→flew
  • Compound: friend + ship = friendship
LT07

Cohesion / Discourse 语篇衔接

  • but (转折核心词): "But flies can't be pets." ×3
  • and: 并列连接
  • then: 时间顺序
  • 代词指代: "he" 指 Fly Guy 还是 Buzz?
  • 结尾衔接: "And so began a beautiful friendship."
LT08

Functional Communication 语用功能

  • Describe: Describe Fly Guy. What is he like?
  • Retell: What happened first / next / at the end?
  • Predict: What will Fly Guy do next?
  • Express opinion: Is a fly a pest or a pet? Why?
  • Compare: Fly Guy vs. a real fly

⏱ 10–20 min · Read Aloud
通读全文 · 理解故事

Chapter 1 精读 (5 min)

页面原文教学重点
p.1–2 "A fly went flying. He was looking for something to eat — something tasty, something slimy." 引入句式 "A ___ went ___-ing"；讨论 fly 想吃什么
p.3–4 "A boy went walking. He was looking for something to catch — something smart, something for The Amazing Pet Show." 平行句式对比；预测男孩要找什么
p.5–6 "The boy caught the fly in a jar. 'A pet!' he said." 理解 catch...in a jar；讨论为什么用 jar
p.7–8 "The fly was mad. He wanted to be free. He stomped his foot and said — BUZZ!" 理解 mad/want to be free；"BUZZ!" 的双关（苍蝇叫声 = 男孩名字）
p.9 "'You know my name! You are the smartest pet in the world!' said Buzz." 理解误会（fly 不是在叫名字）；学习 smartest（最高级）

Chapter 2 精读 (5 min)

页面原文教学重点
p.10–12 Buzz 带 Fly Guy 回家，向爸妈展示 Dad 的反应："Flies can't be pets! They are pests!" 引出 can't be 句式
p.13–14 "He got the fly swatter. The fly cried — BUZZ!" 理解 fly swatter 用途；Buzz 来 rescue
p.15–16 Mom says fly needs a name. Buzz names him "Fly Guy." 命名时刻 — friendship begins
p.17–22 Amazing Pet Show 场景：judge 三次拒绝 反复结构："The fly can do tricks, BUT flies can't be pets." 关注 but 的转折
p.23–28 Fly Guy 的高飞、俯冲、完美入罐 "high, high, high" / "down, down, down" 反复修辞
p.29–30 "This fly is a pet!" — wins award — "a beautiful friendship" 故事结局；讨论 theme: 不被外表判断

⏱ 43–50 min · LT04 Sentence Patterns + Wrap-up
核心句型初探 + 小结

引入本课核心句式，为第二课时深度语法做铺垫：

A [noun] went [verb]-ing.
"A fly went flying." / "A boy went walking."

📌 注意平行结构 — 作者用相同句式讲两个角色的故事。

He was looking for something to [verb].
"He was looking for something to eat." / "He was looking for something to catch."
___ can / can't [verb].
"He can say my name." / "Flies can't be pets."

📌 can 表示"能力"，can't 表示"不能"。这是全书最核心的语法功能。

⏱ 5–18 min · LT05 Grammar
语法功能深度分析

① can / can't — 表达能力 (5 min)

原文句功能结构
"He can say my name."能力（肯定）主语 + can + 动词原形
"Flies can't be pets."能力/可能（否定）主语 + can't + 动词原形
"The fly can do tricks."能力（肯定）主语 + can + 动词原形

练习：用 can/can't 描述 Fly Guy 的能力。
"Fly Guy can ___. Fly Guy can't ___."

③ Superlative 最高级 (3 min)

the + adj-est / the most + adj
"You are the smartest pet in the world!"

smart → smarter → the smartest (短形容词 + est)
beautiful → more beautiful → the most beautiful (长形容词用 most)

LT06 · Morphology 词形变化 (4 min)

引导学生发现词族（word families）：

Base+ ing+ ed+ er / estNotes
flyflying——去 y 加 ing? 不！直接 fly→flying
walkwalkingwalked—规则变化
looklookinglooked—规则变化
stompstompingstomped—规则变化
surprisesurprisingsurprised—去 e 加 -ing/-ed
amazeamazingamazed—去 e 加 -ing/-ed
smart——smarter / smartest单音节直接加
🔍 不规则变化重点 catch → caught (不是 catched) · fly → flew (不是 flyed) · say → said (不是 sayed) · think → thought (不是 thinked)
这些词不能靠规则推导，需要单独记忆！

LT07 · Cohesion 语篇衔接 (4 min)

分析本书中的衔接手段，理解作者如何连接句子和推动故事：

ConnectorFunctionExample from Text
but 转折 contrast — 最重要的衔接词 "The fly can do tricks, but flies can't be pets." (出现 3 次!)
and 并列 addition "And so began a beautiful friendship."
then 顺序 sequence "Then Fly Guy said — BUZZ!"
代词 he 指代 reference — 需要看上下文 "He stomped his foot." → He = the fly
"He got the fly swatter." → He = Dad
💡 讨论：but 的力量 让学生找出所有含 "but" 的句子，讨论：每次 "but" 后面发生了什么变化？
→ "but" 总是带来转折和冲突，推动故事前进。
⏱ 26–40 min · LT08 Functional Communication
口语输出与写作任务

Activity 1: Retell the Story (5 min)

学生两人一组，用以下框架复述故事：

Story Retelling Frame:

First, a fly went flying and a boy went walking.
Then, the boy caught the fly in a jar and named him Fly Guy.
Next, they went to The Amazing Pet Show, but the judges said...
After that, Fly Guy showed his tricks and said...
In the end, Fly Guy won an award and...

Activity 2: Express Your Opinion (5 min)

回到 Big Question，进行 mini-debate：

Discussion Prompts:

  • "Is a fly a pest or a pet? Why do you think so?"
  • "Do you agree with the judges? Why or why not?"
  • "Would you want a fly as a pet? What would you name it?"

Language scaffold:

"I think a fly is a ___ because ___."
"I agree / disagree because ___."
"In my opinion, ___."

Activity 3: Writing — Opinion Piece (4 min 指导 + 课后完成)

Writing Prompt:

Is Fly Guy a pest or a pet? Write your opinion. Give at least two reasons.

Writing Frame (scaffold):

I think Fly Guy is a ___ because ___.
First, ___.
Also, ___.
In conclusion, Fly Guy is ___.
